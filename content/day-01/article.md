# Day 01 · A Library on the Coast

## 英文正文

Twenty years ago, a small town on the **[coast]** was a quiet **[universe]** of its own. People there tried to **[unify]** everything: one market, one school, one opinion. To **[unite]** the town, the leaders built a **[union]** of shops and farms. But the town was **[diverse]**. Not everyone wanted the same kind of life.

Then a young teacher arrived. She was **[versatile]** — she could teach, draw, and fix machines. She wanted to **[convert]** an old factory into a library. Many people were against it. The plan caused a **[revolt]**, and the **[controversy]** lasted a whole year.

**[Contrary]** to what the critics said, the library did not **[reverse]** the town's luck. In **[contrast]**, it improved it. Slowly, ideas began to **[evolve]**. Every evening, children **[revolved]** around the new bookshelves, and their parents got **[involved]**.

The factory used to smell **[nasty]**. The teacher cleaned it and kept a **[formal]** record of every book. She wore a **[uniform]** blue coat, but she never asked anyone to **[conform]**. Instead, she asked the townsfolk to **[formulate]** their own rules. It was a **[reform]** no one had planned. The **[formation]** of a new habit takes time, so she also changed the **[format]** of the weekly meeting. Students **[performed]** short plays. She **[informed]** every family and **[notified]** them of each change.

The result was **[noticeable]**. On the library's first **[anniversary]**, the mayor raised a **[toast]**: "This town finally found its own voice."

## 中文对照

二十年前，海边的一座小镇就是一个安静的**世界**，自成一格。那里的人试图把一切都**统一**起来：一个市场、一所学校、一种观点。为了让小镇**团结**起来，领导人们组建了一个商店与农场的**联合体**。但这座小镇是**多元的**。不是每个人都想要同一种生活。

后来，一位年轻老师来了。她**多才多艺**——能教书、能画画、还能修机器。她想把一座旧工厂**改造成**图书馆。很多人反对。这个计划引发了一场**反抗**，**争议**持续了整整一年。

**与**批评者所说的**相反**，图书馆并没有**逆转**小镇的运气。**相比之下**，它让小镇变好了。慢慢地，观念开始**演变**。每天傍晚，孩子们**围**在新的书架旁，他们的父母也**参与**进来。

那座工厂以前气味很**难闻**。老师把它打扫干净，并为每一本书做了**正式的**记录。她穿着**统一的**蓝色外套，但从不要求任何人**服从**。相反，她请镇上的人自己**制定**规则。这是一场谁都没计划过的**改革**。新习惯的**养成**需要时间，所以她同时改变了每周会议的**形式**。学生们**表演**短剧。她**通知**了每一户人家，把每一项变动都**告知**他们。

效果是**显而易见的**。在图书馆**一周年**纪念日那天，镇长**举杯**致意："这座小镇终于找到了自己的声音。"
