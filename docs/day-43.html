The Old Theatre on River Street河街老剧院

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The old theatre on River Street had been closed for years.

河街上的那座老剧院已经关门好几年了。

Our deputy manager, Ms. Chen, could previse trouble before it arrived.

我们的副经理陈女士能事先预见麻烦。

From the back row she studied the view, and the visual problem was easy to see: the audience seats were too low.

她从后排打量整个视野，视觉上的问题一眼就能看出来：观众席太低了。

She asked an engineer to devise a new plan and to bring his devices and apparatus.

她请一位工程师设计新方案，并带上他的设备和仪器。

He would install air-conditioning above the stage and repair the old projector.

他要给舞台上方装空调，还要修好那台老放映机。

Then came the pretty work.

接下来是些好看的活儿。

We had to furnish the hall with chairs, decorate the walls, and hang one golden ornament over the door.

我们得给大厅配上椅子，装饰墙面，还在门上挂一件金色的装饰品。

On the opening morning we watched the curtain rise.

开演那天早上，我们看着幕布升起。

Small problems arise in any old building, and this one was no different.

任何老楼里都会冒出些小问题，这座楼也不例外。

The manager raised his hand for quiet, and the first song aroused warm applause.

经理举起手示意大家安静，第一首歌就激起了热烈的掌声。

The lights and the music were enough to waken the whole street.

灯光和音乐足以把整条街都唤醒。

By nine o'clock the city was awake.

到九点，整座城市都醒着。

After the show, Ms. Chen wrote a summary for the town office.

演出结束后，陈女士为镇政府写了一份总结。

It had to include every cost and exclude nothing.

它必须包含每一笔开销，什么都不能排除。

To save money we had to eliminate two old lights, and almost every plan was changed except the roof.

为了省钱，我们不得不砍掉两盏旧灯，几乎每个方案都改了，只有屋顶除外。

There was one exception: the golden ornament stayed.

有一个例外：那件金色装饰品留下了。

In the corridor I overheard two young actors.

在走廊里，我偶然听到两个年轻演员说话。

They had undertaken the hardest work, and at first the noise seemed to overwhelm them.

他们承担了最辛苦的活，起初那些噪音似乎要把他们压垮。

"Overall," Ms. Chen concluded, "it worked."

“总的来说，”陈女士得出结论，“它成了。”
