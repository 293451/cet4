The Reading Room on Lantern Street灯笼街上的夜读室

0:00 / 0:00

课文

On Lantern Street the night market had a strict code, and every stall had to regulate its opening hours.

灯笼街的夜市有一套严格的规矩，每个摊位都得自己调节营业时间。

An's aunt kept regular hours there for thirty years, and everyone in the market was related to her.

安的姑姑在那里守了三十年固定的营业时间，市场里每个人都跟她沾着亲。

Their relationship was simple: she sold cheap paperbacks, and they brought her fish and fruit.

她们的关系很简单：姑姑卖便宜的平装书，邻居们给她带鱼和水果。

After the old woman died, An was her only relative at the funeral and had to narrate a life she barely knew.

老太太去世后，安是葬礼上她唯一的亲戚，她只得向一群陌生人讲述一个自己几乎不了解的人生。

All winter she crossed the stone bridge at dusk, while rain soaked through the roof.

整个冬天，她都在黄昏时走过石桥，雨水把屋顶浸透了。

Water penetrated the walls and filtered through the wooden shelves.

水渗进墙壁，又顺着木书架的缝隙渗下去。

For a month An hesitated: should she stay, or replace her aunt's shop with something new?

有一个月，安都在犹豫：是留下来，还是把姑姑的铺子换成别的东西？

She wanted to compensate the neighbours, so she kept the door open.

她想替姑姑补偿街坊们，于是把店门一直开着。

She became a candidate for the market committee, but canceled after one meeting.

她成了市场委员会的候选人，可开了一次会就取消了。

On the first cold night she lit a candle, and a boy chased his lost dog inside.

第一个寒冷的夜里，她点了一支蜡烛，一个男孩追着跑丢的狗冲了进来。

He asked if he could purchase a book with the coins in his pocket.

他问能不能用口袋里的硬币买一本书。

From then on she pursued a plan: a reading room open at night.

从那天起，她追求一个计划：一间开在夜里的小读室。

An old legend said the shop held a mystery, a box nobody could open.

街上有古老的传说说，这铺子里藏着一个谜，一只谁也打不开的箱子。

An did not believe the myth, but she wanted to innovate rather than repair.

安不信这个神话，但她想创新，而不只是修修补补。

The renovation took four months, and the new lights made the little room look warm.

翻新花了四个月，新灯让小小的房间看起来暖融融的。

There was no box, only magazines, a novel with a broken spine, an essay on love, and a romance from 1978.

最后并没有什么箱子，只有一堆杂志、一本断了书脊的小说、一篇写爱情的文章，还有一本1978年买的爱情故事。

She put them on one shelf and wrote: fiction is not a lie.

她把它们摆在同一层书架上，写下一句话：虚构不是谎言。

There was friction at first between An and the neighbours, but by summer three families came every night.

起初安和邻居之间有些摩擦，但到了夏天，有三户人家每晚都来。
