The Sign Said totate牌子上写着 totate

课文

Peter was a carpenter in the village of Wells, and his workshop stood behind the church.

彼得是韦尔斯村里的一名木匠，他的作坊就在教堂后面。

The vicar often came for tea and sat on a wooden chair Peter had made.

牧师常来这儿喝茶，坐在一把彼得亲手做的木椅上。

On the saint's day, every Christian walked up the hill to sing, and Peter's wife wore the long scarf she had knitted herself.

圣徒节那天，村里的每个基督徒都走上山坡去唱歌，彼得的妻子围上了她自己织的那条长围巾。

To the people there, that old building was holy and the ground around it was sacred.

对那里的人来说，那座老建筑是神圣的，周围那片土地也是神圣的。

Behind the workshop Peter grew carrots.

作坊后面，彼得种着胡萝卜。

One wet autumn the carrots began to rot, and when he pulled one up, its root was soft and brown.

有一个多雨的秋天，胡萝卜开始腐烂；他拔起一根，发现根部已经又软又黄。

Beside his bench hung a painted sign, and the first of the two words on it was wrong: totate the wheel.

他工作台旁挂着一块刷了漆的牌子，上面两个词里的第一个还写错了：totate the wheel（本该是 rotate）。

Everyone knew the word should be rotate, but nobody corrected it.

谁都知道那个词本该是 rotate，可从来没人去改。

Peter could build a cart, a carriage or a farm wagon with his own hands, and farmers came from miles away.

彼得能亲手做出一辆手推车、一辆四轮马车或一辆农用大车，农民们从好多英里外赶来。

Then one morning a salesman drove up in a shining automobile.

后来有一天早晨，一位推销员开着一辆锃亮的汽车来了。

Its engine was almost silent, its motor was new, and it drank petrol instead of hay.

它的发动机几乎没有声音，马达是新的，喝的是汽油而不是吃草料。

The salesman slept at the motel on the main road.

那位推销员住在公路边的汽车旅馆。

A coach full of visitors stopped at the gate, and a carrier brought boxes of parts from the city.

一辆长途客车满载游客停在门口，一名运输商从城里运来了几箱配件。

That night the automobile had a breakdown: a long crack had opened in the water tank.

那天夜里，那辆汽车出了故障：水箱上裂开了一道长长的缝。

The driver pressed the horn, and the whole street woke up.

司机按响喇叭，整条街都被吵醒了。

Peter rode home on a horse with an old saddle and decided to buy a vehicle.

彼得骑着配旧马鞍的马回家，决定自己也要买一辆车。

Ten years later a factory automated the whole process, and work that once took a month became automatic.

十年后，一家工厂把整个流程自动化了，过去要一个月才干完的活儿变得几乎不用人动手。

Today a whole fleet of small cars leaves the village every morning.

如今，一整个小车队每天早上从村里开出去。
