A Reporter in the County Court县法院里的记者

0:00 / 0:00

课文

On a wet Tuesday morning, Nina stood on the sidewalk outside the county court and waited for the doors to open.

一个潮湿的星期二早上，妮娜站在县法院外的人行道上，等着大门打开。

She was a print reporter, and this was her first trial.

她是一名纸媒记者，这是她第一次报道庭审。

The case was a small crime: someone had copied another man's blueprint and sold it twice.

案子是一桩小罪案：有人抄了别人的蓝图，把它卖了两次。

Police had found one fingerprint on the paper, but they could not verify whose it was.

警方在纸上找到了一枚指纹，但他们无法核实那是谁的。

A bystander said he had seen a man bypass the back gate, yet he had no proof.

一名旁观者说，他看见有人绕过后门进去，但他没有证据。

Lawyers on both sides appealed to the jury with long speeches.

双方的律师都用长篇陈述向陪审团陈情。

One tried to accuse the other side of hiding evidence, and one tried to excuse his own client.

一方试图控告对方隐瞒证据，另一方试图替自己的当事人开脱。

Nina wrote down every word and could not decide who deserved the blame.

妮娜把每个字都记下来，却分不清该责怪谁。

The charge was serious, and if the jury convicted him, he would go to prison for years.

指控很重，如果陪审团判他有罪，他就要在监狱里待上好几年。

The judge told them not to curse or condemn anyone before they had heard everything.

法官让他们在听完所有陈述之前，不要咒骂或谴责任何人。

A witness described the man's surrender at the police station.

一位证人描述了那人在警察局自首的情形。

The lawyer for the defence argued that discrimination had shaped the case from the start, and that his client had the right to sue.

被告方律师辩称，歧视从一开始就影响了这桩案子，他的当事人有权起诉。

He said the court had no way to justify a long sentence, and that justice meant more than a verdict.

他说，法庭没有理由证明重判是正当的，正义的含义远远不止一个裁决。

The prosecutor asked the jury to be careful and fair.

检察官请陪审团务必谨慎、公正。

After four hours the jury reached its judgement.

四个小时后，陪审团作出了判断。

The verdict was not guilty, and the man was discharged that afternoon.

裁决是无罪，那天下午那人就被释放了。

The state said it would not prosecute again, and would not appeal.

州里表示不会再次起诉，也不会上诉。

Nina closed her notebook.

妮娜合上了笔记本。

Later she said that watching a trial was a kind of torture, but that she had seen how the law worked.

后来她说，旁听一场庭审是一种折磨，但她看清了法律是怎么运转的。
