The Shop That Sold Nothing什么也不卖的店

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Most of us consume far more than we need, and the average consumer never stops to ask why.

我们中大多数人消费的远超所需，而普通消费者从不停下来问为什么。

Every shop invents a custom of its own, and it serves the customer rather than the client.

每家店都会形成自己的惯例，它服务的是顾客而非客户。

The owner was a kind of agent, working for a large agency.

店主算是某种代理人，为一家大机构工作。

Then an alarm went off.

接着警报响了。

It was not a real threat, only a sudden fear that made the whole street feel fierce.

那不是真正的威胁，只是一阵突然的恐惧，让整条街都变得凶险起来。

Nothing could frighten him now, and there was no time to panic.

现在没什么能吓到他了，也来不及惊慌。

Still, he felt uneasy, and he could not ease the feeling.

但他仍感到不安，而且无法让这种感觉缓解。

He grew anxious.

他变得焦虑。

The sign above the door carried his signature, and a small signal light blinked beside it.

门上方的招牌上有他的签名，旁边一盏小信号灯闪着。

He had decided to assign one simple rule, and to resign from the rest.

他决定只定下一条简单的规矩，其余的都辞掉。

To be significant, a shop had to sell something essential, something indispensable.

一家店要有分量，就得卖真正必需的东西，缺它不行的东西。

That was necessary, though not inevitable.

那是必要的，虽然并非不可避免。

He would appoint a new manager and disappoint no one.

他会任命一位新经理，不让任何人失望。

He had to accommodate every kind of visitor, and adapt to each one in turn.

他得容纳各种各样的来客，并逐一去适应他们。
