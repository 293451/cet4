The Jam Factory on Market Avenue市场大道上的果酱厂

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The Lin family's jam factory stood on a wide avenue at the edge of town.

林家果酱厂坐落在镇边一条宽阔的大街上。

Mei had studied economics in the city, and she came home with a bright outlook and new ideas.

梅在城里学过经济学，回家时带着光明的前景和一脑子新想法。

In her first week she had overlooked the price of sugar and the mood of the workers.

头一个星期，她就忽略了两件事：糖的价钱和工人们的心情。

Daily output was three hundred jars, and the family income depended on every one.

厂里每天产出三百罐，全家的收入就靠这一罐一罐。

That winter an outbreak of flu kept half the workers at home, and the outcome looked poor.

那年冬天一场流感暴发，一半工人都留在家里，这一年的结果看起来很糟。

A rival factory across the river began to overtake them, and her uncle, once generous, stopped lending money.

河对岸的对手厂开始赶超他们，一向慷慨的叔叔也不再借钱了。

Mei was not greedy, but she hated to lose.

梅并不贪婪，但她讨厌输。

She worked overtime and stayed in the office until midnight.

她开始加班，在办公室一直待到半夜。

Change did not come overnight, but she was ready to overcome her own pride.

改变不是一夜之间来的，但她已经准备好战胜自己的骄傲。

An old economist on the same avenue gave her one piece of advice: "Be economical. Buy less, fix more."

同一条大街上的一位老经济学家给了她一句建议：“要节俭。少买，多修。”

She found a second-hand machine that was cheaper to operate, and two workers to manipulate the heavy mould with her.

她找到一台二手机器，运转起来更省钱，还请了两名工人和她一起操作沉重的模具。

The old iron bar in the doorway, which had blocked the door for years, was taken away.

门框上那根挡了多年的旧铁条，终于被取了下来。

The barrier between the office and the workshop fell with it, and the two sides began to cooperate.

办公室和车间之间的屏障也随之倒下，两边开始合作。

She asked a neighbouring firm to collaborate on delivery, and together they opened a small outlet.

她请隔壁厂子合作送货，两家一起开了家小小的分销店。

The industry was slow, and productivity rose by a few jars a day, but the overdue debts were paid.

这个行业进展缓慢，生产力每天只多出几罐，但拖欠的债慢慢还清了。

By spring the local economy had turned, and the factory began to produce more jam than ever.

到了春天，当地经济好转，厂里产的果酱比以往任何时候都多。

Gross income was still small, but nobody counted only money.

总收入仍然不多，但家里没人只算钱。
