The Night the Ferry Lost Power渡轮失去动力的那一夜

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The ferry Green Bay leaves the harbour at ten every night.

渡轮「绿湾号」每晚十点驶离港口。

Its crew is small: a captain, an engineer, four sailors.

船上的船员不多：一名船长、一名轮机长，四个水手。

The captain's colleague and the staff in the office on shore keep the radio on all night.

船长的同事和岸上办公室的员工整夜不关无线电。

That night an old fellow with a dog came aboard.

那天夜里，一位带着狗的老头上船了。

His stuff slid across the deck when the sea turned rough.

海面转糟时，他的行李在甲板上滑来滑去。

At midnight the engine made a sound that resembled a hammer on metal.

午夜时分，发动机发出了一种像锤子敲在金属上的声音。

One loose screw had fallen out of the pump.

一颗松动的螺丝从水泵上掉了下来。

In his haste the engineer grabbed the wrong tool.

轮机长一着急，抓错了工具。

He had always despised that pump and looked at it with contempt.

他一直看不上那台水泵，此刻更是带着蔑视盯着它。

The captain urged the passengers to stay in their seats.

船长催所有乘客坐回自己的座位。

With great caution he turned toward the nearest island, aware that the wind would rise.

他万分谨慎地把船头转向最近的岛，同时意识到一点以后风会变大。

He had taken one precaution: the tanks were full.

他提前做过一项预防措施：油箱是满的。

On deck the crew stayed alert and conscious of the cold.

甲板上，船员们保持警觉，也清楚地感到寒冷。

Then the lights went out and the passengers felt real terror.

接着灯灭了，乘客们感到了真正的恐惧。

A little girl was terrified by the black water and turned pale.

一个小女孩被漆黑的海水吓坏了，脸色变得苍白。

Her mother held her tighter and stared in horror.

她母亲把她搂得更紧，惊恐地盯着海面。

It was a dreadful night.

那是个可怕的夜晚。

The old fellow wanted to rebel, but his conscience held him in his seat.

那位老头想要反抗，但良心把他按在了座位上。

The warm cabin tempted the sleepy passengers, yet nobody slept.

温暖的船舱诱惑着困倦的乘客，可谁也没睡。

A warning on the engine-room door read paim, where the painter had meant palm.

轮机舱的门上有一行手写的警告，写的是 paim，写字的人本想写 palm。

The engineer made one last attempt, but it was in vain.

轮机长做了最后一次尝试，但徒劳无功。

Then the captain found a red triangle on the map, marking a small calm bay.

这时船长在地图上找到一个红色的三角形，那是一个小小的平静海湾。

By three o'clock both engines worked in harmony.

到三点钟，两台发动机协调地一起运转起来。

The hatred on the young sailor's face turned into a smile.

年轻水手脸上的憎恨慢慢变成了笑容。

In the morning the staff on shore counted everyone twice.

早上，岸上的员工把人数点了两遍。
