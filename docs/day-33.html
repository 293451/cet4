The Print Shop Beside the Highway公路边的小印刷店

0:00 / 0:00

课文

In Millbrook, a small print shop stood beside the old highway, and the pressure of traffic never dropped all day.

在米尔布鲁克，一家小印刷店立在老公路旁边，白天的车流压力从未减轻。

Ada was a technician by training and a skilled mechanic, and she could fix any mechanism in the shop with her own hands.

阿达是科班出身的技术员，也是个熟练的机械修理工，店里任何机件她都能亲手修好。

Her finest skill was setting type, a technique her father had taught her, and she trusted no new technology to replace it.

她最拿手的技艺是排版，这是父亲教她的方法，她不相信有什么新技术能取代它。

Even a young technologist from a biotechnology company in the city once came to watch her hands.

就连城里一家生物科技公司的年轻技术专家，也曾来看她干活的手。

Above her desk hung an old radar set and a box of hardware, and she joked that they gave off more radiation than the sun.

她书桌上方挂着一台旧雷达设备和一箱工具，她开玩笑说它们发出的辐射比太阳还多。

A drawer under the counter held her manuscript, forty pages about the road.

柜台下的一个抽屉里放着她的手稿，四十页，写的都是那条路。

The script was plain, and she had written it by hand one winter.

手迹很朴素，是她用一个冬天手写出来的。

A doctor down the street said he would prescribe rest for her, but she never rested.

街那头的一位医生说他愿意给她开休息的处方，可她从来没有休息过。

Then the state announced a new motorway and a freeway through the valley, and an expressway link was added to the plan.

后来州里宣布要修一条穿过山谷的高速公路和快速路，方案里还加了一条高速连接线。

Reporters from the press arrived to ask questions, and the whole town was full of noise.

新闻界的记者赶来提问，整个小镇闹哄哄的。

To buy new equipment Ada had to overdraw her account at the bank, and she went there herself to withdraw the last of her savings.

为了买新设备，阿达不得不透支银行账户，她还亲自去银行取出了最后一点存款。

At the meeting, an official tried to repress the small protest and to suppress the questions people asked.

会上，一名官员试图镇压这场小小的抗议，压制人们提出的问题。

Ada felt oppressed by the silence, and she could not compress her anger into a single line.

阿达被这种沉默压得难受，她无法把怒气压缩成一行字。

"I must express what I think," she said.

“我必须表达我的想法，”她说。

"You cannot press a whole valley into a road, and you cannot depress us into silence."

“你不能把整条山谷压成一条路，也不能压得我们不敢出声。”

Her photographs, printed the next morning, did impress everyone who saw them.

第二天早上印出来的照片，确实让每个看到的人都印象深刻。
