The Street Where the Parade Passed游行经过的那条街

0:00 / 0:00

课文

On the main street there was one bakery and one old barber shop.

镇上的主街上有一家面包房和一家老理发店。

Behind the counter stood Sarah, the only female baker in the town.

柜台后面站的是萨拉，镇上唯一的女面包师。

Her brother was the only male in the family who could not cook.

她哥哥是家里唯一一个完全不会做饭的男性。

Within a year, everyone said her bread had become mainstream in the town.

一年之内，大家都说她的面包成了镇上的主流。

Steam rose from the big pot, and water ran down the wall in a small stream.

大锅上冒着蒸汽，水顺着墙流成一条小小的溪流。

When the oven door opened by mistake, the children outside would scream.

烤箱门一开错，外面的孩子就会尖叫。

On Sundays she added cream and butter to the dough.

星期天她会往面团里加奶油和黄油。

In summer the water in the pot would slowly evaporate.

夏天一热，锅里的水就会慢慢蒸发掉。

That summer, a strange germ appeared in the river.

那年夏天，河里出现了一种奇怪的病菌。

People said the water carried a virus, and bacteria from the farm had got in.

人们说水里带着病毒，农场的细菌进了河。

Sarah had a strong immune system, but many others fell ill.

萨拉的免疫系统很强，但很多其他人都病倒了。

A small bug bit her arm, and an insect flew into the flour.

一只小虫子咬了她的胳膊，一只昆虫飞进了面粉里。

She found a worm in an old apple and threw it out.

她在一个旧苹果里发现了一条虫子，就把它扔了。

Her appearance at the town meeting surprised everyone.

她出现在镇民大会上，让所有人都吃了一惊。

Some shops closed, and the small ones seemed to vanish.

有些店关了门，那些小店仿佛消失了。

Sarah had to balance the books of the bakery every night.

萨拉每天晚上都得把面包房的账目做平衡。

Her life and her mother's life had run in parallel lines, she said.

她说，她的生活和母亲的生活曾经是平行的两条线。

The paradox was that the busiest week of the year was also the sickest.

矛盾之处在于，一年里最忙的那一周也是最多人病倒的一周。

In October a great parade filled the street, and the whole town came out to march behind the band.

十月，一场盛大的游行占满了街道，全镇人都出来跟着乐队行进。

For one afternoon the street looked like paradise.

有那么一个下午，这条街看起来就像天堂一样。

The sky above the roofs opened like heaven itself, though the tired shop owners called it a small hell.

屋顶上方的天空像天堂一样敞开了，不过累坏了的店主说这一天真是活受罪。

Two men had to heave a heavy drum onto a truck.

两个男人只好把一只沉重的鼓使劲抬上卡车。
