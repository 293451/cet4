The Ship in the Grey Ice灰冰上的那艘船

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The research ship lay still, surrounded by grey ice.

那艘科考船一动不动地停着，四周全是灰白色的冰。

The climate there changed faster than anywhere on earth, and the mood on board changed with it.

那里的气候变化比地球上任何地方都快，船上的气氛也跟着变。

Under such circumstances, the captain kept a strict circle of habits.

在这种情形下，船长守着一整套固定的习惯。

Every morning the crew ran one circuit of the deck, and warm air circulated through the cabins.

每天早上，船员们都要绕甲板跑一圈，暖空气在船舱里循环流动。

When the ice cracked, the whole ship would leap like a nervous animal.

冰一裂开，整条船就像受惊的动物一样猛地跳起来。

The captain conducted the journey from a small room behind the bridge.

船长在驾驶台后面的小舱里指挥整段航程。

He could direct the ship with one hand: a short command, a clear direction.

他一只手就能指挥这条船：一句简短的命令，一个明确的方向。

He instructed the young sailors in a low voice, and no one argued.

他低声指导年轻的水手，没有人顶嘴。

He had been in the military for twenty years.

他在军队里待了二十年。

Each evening the radio officer would broadcast the weather.

每天晚上，报务员都会播报天气。

His forecast was never perfect.

他的预报从来都不完美。

We cannot foresee the ice, he said; we can only guess.

“我们没法预见冰情，”他说，“只能猜。”

From the fore part of the ship the lookout watched the white distance, and land came into sight at last.

在船的前部，瞭望员盯着白茫茫的远方，终于看见了陆地。

There was no time for sightseeing.

没有时间观光。

The captain had to oversee the whole deck, and his one oversight was a forgotten rope.

船长必须监督整个甲板，而他唯一的一次疏忽，是一根被遗忘的绳子。

His insight was simple: the ice always tells the truth.

他的洞察很简单：冰从不说谎。

He pushed forward, and the ship moved forth into open water.

他向前推进，船驶进了开阔的水域。

A small camera was on board to capture pictures of the ice.

船上有一台小相机，用来拍下冰的样子。

The captain was capable of reading them better than any scientist.

船长解读这些照片的能力，比任何科学家都强。

The hold had the capacity to carry two hundred boxes of samples, and for this small crew the ship itself was their capital.

货舱能装两百箱样本，而对这几个船员来说，这条船本身就是他们的资本。
