The Old Factory by the Cape海角边的旧工厂

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Gull Point was a small town on a bare cape.

鸥角是一个小镇，坐落在一片光秃秃的海角上。

The scenery was rough but beautiful, and the landscape drew visitors all year.

景色粗犷却好看，这片风景一年到头都吸引着游客。

At the north end of the town stood an old factory, empty for twenty years.

镇子北头立着一座旧工厂，空了二十年。

The landlord who owned the land wanted to pull it down.

拥有这块地的房东想把厂子拆掉。

A young painter named Lin had a different plan.

一位名叫林的年轻画家却另有打算。

It was, she said, an ideal place for a gallery, and the whole town could use it.

她说，这里是开画廊的理想之地，全镇的人都能用。

Her plan was not perfect, but it was thorough.

她的方案谈不上完美，但很周密。

Behind the factory there was an old laboratory, and next to it a lavatory.

工厂后面有一间旧实验室，旁边是一间盥洗室。

Nearby stood a small grocery shop and an old nursery for children.

再过去是一家小杂货店，还有一所收孩子的托儿所。

The landlord did not like the idea, and he did not retreat.

房东不喜欢这个主意，他并没有退让。

In the local paper, two writers attacked each other for a month.

当地报纸上，两个写手互相攻击了整整一个月。

Some people said tourists would invade their quiet streets.

有人说游客会侵扰他们安静的街道。

Others said a stranger had no right to intrude.

也有人说，一个外人不该闯进来。

Lin did not want to fight; she wanted to negotiate.

林不想争，她想谈。

She invited the landlord to her kitchen and fried him a fish from the cape.

她请房东到自家厨房，给他煎了一条从海角钓来的鱼。

In the morning they wrote a treaty on a piece of paper.

第二天早上，他们在一张纸上写下了一纸约定。

The landlord would keep the land, and Lin would use the buildings.

房东保留土地，林使用房子。

She launched the work in March and initiated a hundred small repairs.

三月她启动了工程，发起了上百处小修小补。

It was all her own initiative, and she utilized every old machine she found.

这全是她自己的主动，她利用了能找到的每一台旧机器。

The winter wind off the sea was deadly cold, but the work went on.

海上吹来的冬风冷得要命，但活儿没停。

Slowly the factory conquered the town's heart, and a colony of artists moved in.

慢慢地，工厂征服了全镇人的心，一群艺术家搬了进来。

On the opening night it was a triumph for Lin and a victory for the whole town.

开幕那晚，对林来说是一次巨大的成功，对整个小镇来说是一场胜利。

The empty territory had come alive.

这片空着的地方活了过来。
