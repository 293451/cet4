Day 53 · 12/4 · Unit 8 第 1561-1590 词

The Restorer and the Queen修复师与女王

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The rules of the palace workshop were rigid: no food, no music, and no talking after eight.

宫廷作坊的规矩很死板：不准吃东西，不准放音乐，八点以后不许说话。

Elena's teacher was harsh, and some of the other restorers called him cruel, even brutal.

埃莱娜的老师很严厉，别的修复师里有人说他残酷，甚至残暴。

Once a frame fell from the wall and left a bruise on her arm and a long wound in her hand.

有一次一个画框从墙上掉下来，在她手臂上砸出淤青，在手上划出一道很深的伤口。

She was his only dependant, so she could not simply walk away.

她是老师唯一要养活的人，所以没法一走了之。

For six months she was isolated in the north room, learning to navigate the dark stairs with a lamp.

整整六个月，她一个人被隔离在北屋，学着提一盏灯走过那些漆黑的楼梯。

The floor vibrated each time the great doors opened, and she sent her reports to the office via a young guard.

每次大门打开，地板都会震动；她的报告则托一个年轻卫兵带出去。

The work itself was a portrait of a queen: her crown, her royal dress, and a regal, upright face.

这幅作品本身是一位女王的肖像：她的王冠、她的王室礼服，还有一张端庄挺直的脸。

Elena had to stay loyal to the first painter and faithful to every brush mark.

埃莱娜必须忠于最初的那位画家，忠实于每一道笔触。

The queen stood vertical and still in the frame, noble and calm.

画里的女王垂直而立，一动不动，高贵而平静。

The painting had a fine reputation, though its fame had faded long ago.

这幅画名声很好，虽然它的名气早已淡去。

The palace wanted its prestige back, and the old teacher wanted the esteem of the city.

宫殿想找回它的声望，老教师想得到全城的敬重。

He told Elena that real merit is not praise but virtue, and that a moral restorer must be honest in small things.

他告诉埃莱娜，真正的价值不是赞美而是美德，一个有道德的修复师在小事上也必须诚实。

One night a violent storm shook the palace, and someone tried to violate the seals on the door.

一天夜里，一场猛烈的暴风雨摇撼着宫殿，有人试图破坏门上的封条。

By morning a long queue of visitors waited at the gate to see the queen.

到早上，一长队游客在门口排队，等着看那位女王。
