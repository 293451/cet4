Day 03 · 10/15 · Unit 3 第 61-90 词

The Rules of the Workshop车间的规矩

0:00 / 0:00

课文

A short troop of workers gathered at the gate.

一小队工人聚集在门口。

What would precede the morning shift were a few simple rules.

早班之前，是几条简单的规矩。

The whole process was written down on a board, and the procedure had not changed in twenty years.

整个过程写在一块板上，这套流程二十年没变过。

Access to the tools was free, but only to those who signed the book.

工具是随便用的，但只有签名登记的人才能用。

Anyone who tried to exceed the daily limit would find a small fine instead.

谁想超出每日限额，换来的是一笔小额罚款。

Beyond that first warning, there was an excess charge on the pay slip.

越过第一次警告之后，工资单上会多一笔超额费用。

Old habits had to recede, and the manager was not willing to concede.

旧习惯必须退去，而经理不愿意让步。

So they reached a compromise between speed and care.

于是他们在速度与谨慎之间达成了折中。

Success, he said, would not come from one big push.

他说，成功不会来自一次猛冲。

Succeeding meant repeating small acts until they stuck.

所谓成功，就是重复小的动作，直到它们固定下来。

In quick succession, the workers learned each task.

工人们一个接一个地迅速学会了每项任务。

Each young successor to an older hand was paired with a teacher, and those who excel at their craft taught the rest.

每个接替老师傅的年轻继任者都配了一位师傅，而那些手艺出众的人则去教其余的人。

Then came the hardest part.

接下来是最难的部分。

The floor was not static.

地面不是静止的。

A machine does not move on its own.

机器不会自己动。

Motion needs a motive, and a wage alone rarely succeeds in motivating anyone for long.

运动需要动机，而单靠工资很少能长久地激励任何人。

So he began to remove the dull parts, and used emotion as a tool rather than a problem.

于是他开始去掉那些枯燥的环节，把情绪当作工具而不是问题。

It was rational to do so, and the ratio of finished work to wasted time rose sharply.

这样做是理性的，成品与浪费时间的比率大幅上升。

He kept a remote corner of the workshop as a place to think.

他把车间一个偏僻的角落留作思考的地方。

Promote one of them, and the others would follow.

提拔他们中的一个，其余的人就会跟上。

Action, he said, always beats talk.

他说，行动永远胜过空谈。

Behave as if the work matters, and stay active.

表现得像这份工作很重要，并保持主动。

Then activate the same rule in your own head.

然后，把同一条规则在你自己的脑子里激活。
