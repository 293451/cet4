The Shop on the Frontier边境上的铺子

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Marlow is a small town on the frontier, right on the rim of the sea.

马洛是一座边境上的小镇，就坐落在海边。

A low hedge runs along the boundary of the square, and the fields stretch to the horizon.

一道矮树篱沿着广场的边界延伸，篱笆那边，田野一直铺到地平线。

From the edge of the square you can see the whole harbour.

从广场边上望出去，整个港口尽收眼底。

Old Mr. Bai had kept a shop there for forty years.

老白先生在那儿开了四十年的铺子。

Every morning he carried his telescope up to the roof and watched the ships come in.

每天早上他都把望远镜搬上屋顶，看着进港的船。

"I can only speculate," he said, "but that one is bound for the north."

“我只能推测，”他说，“不过那艘船是往北去的。”

From his perspective, nothing in the square was hidden.

从他的角度看，广场上没有什么藏得住的东西。

The red roof of his shop was the most conspicuous thing in town.

他那红屋顶是全镇最引人注目的东西。

He imported cloth and exported tea.

他进口布匹，出口茶叶。

Half his goods he sold wholesale, half at retail, and he was the only seller of English thread in the county.

他的货一半批发，一半零售，而且他是全县唯一卖英国线的人。

He advertised nothing at all.

他从不做广告。

"My prices speak for me," he said.

“我的价钱会替我说话，”他说。

"This town has prospered for a hundred years."

“这座镇子已经繁荣了一百年。”

A young porter carried the bags from the ferry, and a tailor knew every detail of every coat.

一个年轻的搬运工把行李从渡轮上扛下来，裁缝清楚每一件外套的每一个细节。

Travelers showed their passports at the gate and then walked into the square.

旅客在门口出示护照，然后走进广场。

That spring the market began to slump.

那年春天，市场开始萧条。

The bumper of a delivery truck bumped the hedge, and a lump of wet clay fell into the road.

一辆送货车的保险杠撞上了树篱，一块湿泥巴掉到了路上。

Apples bounced out of a box and rolled across the square.

苹果从箱子里弹出来，滚过广场。

In the afternoon the old man found a small bomb beside the well.

下午，老人在井边发现了一枚小炸弹。

"It is only a joke," someone said.

“只是个玩笑，”有人说。

He did not laugh.

他没有笑。

He asked two men to brace the shop wall, and then he stood at the margin of the square.

他叫两个人用木头撑住铺子的墙，然后站在广场边缘。

01 speculate /'spekjulert/ v. 推测，推断；投机
