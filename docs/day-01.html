A Library on the Coast海边的一座图书馆

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Twenty years ago, a small town on the coast was a quiet universe of its own.

二十年前，海边的一座小镇就是一个安静的世界，自成一格。

People there tried to unify everything: one market, one school, one opinion.

那里的人试图把一切都统一起来：一个市场、一所学校、一种观点。

To unite the town, the leaders built a union of shops and farms.

为了让小镇团结起来，领导人们组建了一个商店与农场的联合体。

But the town was diverse.

但这座小镇是多元的。

Not everyone wanted the same kind of life.

不是每个人都想要同一种生活。

Then a young teacher arrived.

后来，一位年轻老师来了。

She was versatile. She could teach, draw, and fix machines.

她多才多艺。能教书、能画画、还能修机器。

She wanted to convert an old factory into a library.

她想把一座旧工厂改造成图书馆。

Many people were against it.

很多人反对。

The plan caused a revolt, and the controversy lasted a whole year.

这个计划引发了一场反抗，争议持续了整整一年。

Contrary to what the critics said, the library did not reverse the town's luck.

与批评者所说的相反，图书馆并没有逆转小镇的运气。

In contrast, it improved it.

相比之下，它让小镇变好了。

Slowly, ideas began to evolve.

慢慢地，观念开始演变。

Every evening, children revolved around the new bookshelves, and their parents got involved.

每天傍晚，孩子们围在新的书架旁，他们的父母也参与进来。

The factory used to smell nasty.

那座工厂以前气味很难闻。

The teacher cleaned it and kept a formal record of every book.

老师把它打扫干净，并为每一本书做了正式的记录。

She wore a uniform blue coat, but she never asked anyone to conform.

她穿着统一的蓝色外套，但从不要求任何人服从。

Instead, she asked the townsfolk to formulate their own rules.

相反，她请镇上的人自己制定规则。

It was a reform no one had planned.

这是一场谁都没计划过的改革。

The formation of a new habit takes time, so she also changed the format of the weekly meeting.

新习惯的养成需要时间，所以她同时改变了每周会议的形式。

Students performed short plays.

学生们表演短剧。

She informed every family and notified them of each change.

她通知了每一户人家，把每一项变动都告知他们。

The result was noticeable.

效果是显而易见的。

On the library's first anniversary, the mayor raised a toast.

在图书馆一周年纪念日那天，镇长举杯致意。

This town finally found its own voice.

这座小镇终于找到了自己的声音。
