The Small School in the Valley山谷里的小学校

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Elena taught English in a small school in a valley, and she was fluent in three languages.

埃琳娜在山谷里一所小学校教英语，她能流利地说三种语言。

Her mother had been a teacher too, and Elena inherited the school, part of the village heritage.

她母亲也曾是老师，埃琳娜继承了这所学校，它也是村里遗产的一部分。

By local convention, every family sent a child to her evening class.

按当地习俗，每家都送一个孩子来上她的夜课。

The classroom was compact, and when snow slid off the roof, the impact shook the wall.

教室很紧凑，雪从屋顶滑下来时，那一下撞击震得墙壁直响。

Good firewood was scarce that winter, and English books were rare in the village.

那年冬天好柴火很缺，村里英语书也很少见。

Her influence on those children could not be measured.

她对那些孩子的影响无法估量。

In the winter of that year, influenza came to the valley.

那年冬天，流感来到了山谷。

A doctor from the city exerted all his strength, but the clinic had few supplies.

城里来的医生用尽全身力气，但诊所的物资少得可怜。

Elena's family was not affluent, yet she never asked for money.

埃琳娜家并不富裕，但她从不向人要钱。

For two weeks the mood in the classroom was tense.

有两个星期，教室里的气氛很紧张。

She kept up an intensive reading programme, and the work was intense.

她坚持高强度的阅读计划，对每个人来说功课都很紧张。

A good teacher needs a sense of what a child really needs.

好老师需要对孩子的真实需要有感觉。

She was sensitive to every small change in their faces.

她对孩子们脸上的每一点细小变化都很敏感。

Her commonsense told her to slow down, and her sensible plan was ten pages a day.

她的常识告诉她该放慢节奏，于是她明智的计划变成了一天只读十页。

Some parents called her method nonsense, but the children improved.

有些家长说她这套是胡说八道，但孩子们进步了。

In spring the snow melted, and a warm wind came over the fields.

春天雪化了，一阵暖风从田野上吹来。

Elena even tamed a small wild bird that lived near the window.

埃琳娜甚至驯服了一只住在窗边的小野鸟。

The sun is the source of light, and time is her most precious resource.

太阳是光的来源，而时间是她最宝贵的资源。

She often spoke about mental health and about the fundamental rule of her school.

她常谈起心理健康，也常谈起她学校的根本原则。

A mistake in grammar is never fatal, she said.

她说，语法上的错误从来都不致命。

In total, forty pupils passed the exam that June.

那年六月，一共有四十名学生通过了考试。

Now she keeps every record in digital form.

现在她把所有记录都以数字形式保存。

Her greatest talent, the villagers said, was patience.

村里人说，她最大的才能是耐心。
