The House of Wet Clay湿黏土屋

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Umi lived in a small cottage at the edge of the rain forest.

乌米住在雨林边上的一间小屋。

The air there was always humid, and in the tropics the heat was heavy.

那里的空气总是潮湿，在热带，热气沉沉的。

After the rain the ground stayed moist for days, and the sheets in her room were damp and heavy.

雨后地面会一连几天都是湿的，她房里的被单又潮又重。

Only the early morning brought a chill.

只有清晨才有一点凉意。

In that house, water and clay were vital; without them she could not work.

在那间屋子里，水和黏土至关重要；没有它们她就没法干活。

She worked hard to attain one small skill each week, and she was happy to obtain a bag of good clay.

她每周努力学到一个小本领，能买到一袋好黏土她就很高兴。

Water will not retain its shape, so she had to maintain the fire under the pot all day.

水保不住形状，所以她得整天让锅下的火不灭。

A bowl of rice was enough to sustain her, and the forest seemed to pertain to a world she would never know.

一碗米饭就够她维持生活了，而那片森林仿佛属于一个她永远不会了解的世界。

In the evening she would entertain visitors with tea and stories.

傍晚她会给来客端上茶，讲讲故事，招待他们。

Once, heavy rain detained her at the market until dark.

有一次大雨把她在集市上耽搁到天黑。

Another time, a thief was arrested near her door.

还有一次，一个贼在她门附近被逮捕了。

She made little figures of every species she saw in the forest.

她给在森林里见到的每一个物种都做了小塑像。

Each creature had its own shape, and she was a creative artist in her own quiet way.

每只生物都有自己的形状，而她自己就是一个安静的、有创造力的艺术家。

Her hands could give a concrete shape to soft clay.

她的双手能给柔软的黏土一个具体的形状。

She believed every human being carried a story, and that humankind had always done the same.

她相信每个人都带着一个故事，而人类一直和她做着同样的事。

She was humble about her own work, and she never lost her humour when a figure broke.

她对自己的作品很谦逊，塑像碎了也从不忘幽默一下。

Each individual piece was different, and no two were ever the same.

每一件单独的作品都不一样，从来没有两件是相同的。

She would divide the clay into small balls, then subdivide one ball into four parts.

她会把黏土分成小球，再把一个球细分成四份。

The wet weather could damage a finished figure, and long rain would render the garden useless.

潮湿的天气会损害做好的塑像，长时间的雨会让园子变得一无用处。

01 vital /'vaitl/ adj. 至关重要的;必不可少的
课文water and clay were vital
记六个头的(vi是罗马数字六)， 说明这个人很有头脑，他的地位一定是“至关重 要的”。

族vitality n. 活力 / vitamin n. 维生素
02 attain /ə'tein/ v. 达到;获得

真题We expect our pupils to do their best, and we give them every encouragement to attain this goal. 我们期望我们的学生尽他们最 大的努力，我们也会鼓励他们来实现这一目标。
族attainment n. 成就

族retention n. 保留

根main(主要) + tain(拿) → 一直把住 → 维持

族maintenance n. 维护

根sus(在下面) + tain(拿) → 从下面托住
真题Some of those small mammals help to sustain such big birds as golden eagles. 有些小 型哺乳动物有助于饲养像金雕这样的大鸟类。 (2019)
