The Studio in the Old Barn旧谷仓里的摄影棚

课文

Every October, the harvest brought the whole valley together.

每年十月，收割把整个山谷的人都聚到一起。

The yield from those small fields was never large, but it was enough for everyone.

那几小块地的产量从来不高，但对每个人来说都够用了。

By the time the rice was mature, the farmers had already ploughed the next field.

等稻子成熟时，农民们已经把下一块地犁好了。

Farming was still the dominant way of life in the village.

在村里，种地仍然是占主导地位的生活方式。

In the house, however, the work was different.

然而，屋子里的活计就完全不同了。

Cooking, washing and cleaning were endless domestic chores.

做饭、洗衣、打扫，是没完没了的家务。

They were dull and tedious, and after a few hours they made anyone feel dumb.

这些活又枯燥又乏味，干上几个钟头，谁都会觉得自己变笨了。

So Li Wei made a schedule.

于是李薇给自己排了一张时间表。

Her scheme was simple: finish the chores before noon, then work on her own project.

她的方案很简单：中午前干完家务，然后做自己的事。

She wrote it on the first page of her agenda and turned it into a daily routine.

她把它写在记事本的第一页上，慢慢变成了一种日常习惯。

Her project was the old barn behind the house.

她的项目是屋后的旧谷仓。

She walked through the narrow passage at the back and studied the interior and the exterior.

她穿过后面那条窄窄的通道，仔细看了看里面和外面。

The external walls were grey stone; the internal walls were dark wood.

外墙是灰色的石头，内墙是深色的木头。

She worked for the local paper, a small medium with a young readership.

她给当地一家报纸干活，那是一家面向年轻读者的小媒体。

Her photographs were only of intermediate quality then, and the media paid little attention to her.

那时她的照片只算中等水平，媒体几乎没注意过她。

Still, she had to locate a good site for a studio.

不过，她得先给摄影棚找一个好位置。

She decided to allocate half her savings to the roof and to situate the darkroom in the corner near the window.

她决定把一半积蓄分给屋顶，把暗房安置在靠窗的角落。

When she photographed the farmers, she asked them to pose among the rice.

给农民拍照时，她请他们站在稻田里摆姿势。

She corrected their posture, then bent down to expose the film to the morning light.

她纠正他们的姿态，然后蹲下去，让胶片在晨光中曝光。
