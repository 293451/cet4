课文

Nina's grandfather built the old wooden cottage in 1960.

妮娜的祖父在 1960 年亲手盖了那间老木屋。

He used raw boards and crude tools, and every summer he reaped the fruit.

他用的是生木板和粗陋的工具，每年夏天他都会收获果实。

By August the apples were ripe and heavy on the branches.

到了八月，苹果熟了，沉甸甸地挂在枝上。

Nina wanted to transform the cottage into a small kitchen for travellers.

妮娜想把这间小屋改造成给旅人做饭的小餐馆。

She had to transplant two young trees nearer the wall.

她得把两棵小树移植到靠墙一些的地方。

A truck would transport the old furniture to the city, and she would transfer the money to her cousin.

一辆卡车会把旧家具运到城里，她则会把钱转给表姐。

Every transaction went into a paper book.

每一笔交易都记在一个纸本子上。

She could translate the menu into French for one guest.

她能为一位客人把菜单翻译成法语。

A small radio transmitted the weather report every morning.

一台小收音机每天早上播送天气预报。

Trucks in transit through the village often stopped for coffee.

过境经过村子的卡车常常停下来喝咖啡。

The new windows were transparent, but the old door was still opaque.

新窗户是透明的，但旧门还是不透明。

Her success soon became apparent to everyone.

她的成功很快就对每个人都变得显而易见了。

It was obvious that the kitchen would grow: the evidence was the long line of cars.

小餐馆会扩张是明显的：证据就是门外排起的长长车队。

The change was evident even to her grandfather.

这一变化连她祖父都看得出来。

He would assert that the soil here was the best in the country.

他总断言这里的土壤是全国最好的。

The land became a desert in July.

七月里这片地几乎成了沙漠。

She would insert a thin stick into the ground to test the soil.

她会往地里插一根细棍来试土。

Some roots would exsert from the dry earth like small fingers.

一些根会像小手指一样从干土里伸出来。

For dessert she served apples with cream.

甜点她端上的是苹果配奶油。

Her grandfather had deserted the city long ago.

她祖父很久以前就抛弃了城市。

In spring, white flowers would bloom at the edge of the orchard.

春天，果园边上会开满白花。

That year a quiet boom began: three new shops opened.

那一年，一股热潮悄悄兴起：三家新店开了张。

In autumn the kitchen's future began to loom like a heavy cloud.

到了秋天，小餐馆的前途开始像一片乌云一样逼近。

The long winter afternoons were full of gloom.

漫长的冬日午后充满了忧郁。

Her grandfather's praise did boost her confidence.

祖父的夸奖确实增强了她的信心。

He would boast that the apple pie had no equal anywhere.

他会吹嘘说，这苹果派别处哪儿也比不上。
