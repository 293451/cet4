The Oath and the Glass Case誓言与玻璃柜

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Dr. Qiao began work at the small hospital in Anhe on a cold Monday, and on her first morning she had to swear an oath before the staff.

乔医生在一个寒冷的周一开始在安和镇的小医院上班，头一天早上她得在全院职工面前宣誓。

She pledged to treat every patient the same way and to assure each of them that he was safe.

她保证对每个病人都一视同仁，也答应让每个人都确信自己是安全的。

"We cannot insure you against illness," the old physician said.

“我们没法给你保住了不得病的险，”老内科医生说。

"But we can ensure you are never alone, and I guarantee nobody here will lie to you."

“但我们能确保你永远不会一个人待着，我担保这里没人会对你说假话。”

In the corridor stood a glass case.

走廊上立着一个玻璃柜。

Inside it lay a sword and an arrow that a farmer had pulled out of the river.

里面放着一把剑和一支箭，是一位农民从河里捞上来的。

One night a boy of twelve was carried in.

一天夜里，一个十二岁的男孩被送了进来。

He said the ceiling lamp dazzled him, the room began to turn, and he felt dizzy.

他说天花板上的灯晃得他眼花，屋子开始打转，他头晕得厉害。

A minute later he fainted.

一分钟后就昏了过去。

Dr. Qiao asked about his meals and gave him a physical check.

乔医生问他平时吃什么，又给他做了一次身体检查。

His stomach had begun to swell, and it hurt when she pressed it.

他的肚子已经开始肿起来，按下去就疼。

She took a sample of his blood and sent a specimen to the lab.

她抽了一份血样，把标本送到了化验室。

The report came back: it was not a contagion, and he had no fever.

报告回来了：不是传染病，也没有发烧。

She could diagnose the problem at once.

她一下子就能诊断出问题所在。

A body is a living organism, and each organ has its function.

人体是一个活的有机体，每个器官都有自己的功能。

When the intake of sugar is too high, the stomach cannot absorb it or digest it.

糖的摄入量太高时，胃既吸收不了也消化不了。

He did not need surgery.

他不需要做手术。

The surgeon on duty said a bowl of rice would help more than his knife.

值班的外科医生说，一碗米饭比他的刀管用得多。

A nurse came to organize and deliver his meals to his bed.

一位护士来安排他的饮食，并把饭送到床边。

For three days he drank warm water through a straw.

头三天他只喝温水，用一根吸管。

On the fourth morning he walked out, and Dr. Qiao gave him a small dose of medicine.

第四天早上他走出去了；乔医生给他开了一小剂药。
