The Clockmaker's Shop钟表匠的铺子

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The shop sold currency from every age, and its clocks were modern and up-to-date.

这家铺子卖各个年代的货币，它的钟既现代又时新。

Fashion changed, but a good clock did not follow it.

时尚在变，但一只好钟不跟着变。

The building itself was old, once a temple, and later a temporary market.

这栋楼本身很老，曾是座庙，后来成了临时集市。

Today it was a contemporary shop with the same old walls, and the space inside was abundant.

如今它是一家当代的铺子，墙还是那面老墙，里面的空间很充裕。

A mass of boxes sat in the corner, and one massive cabinet leaned against the wall.

角落里堆着一大堆盒子，一个大柜子靠着墙。

The owner gave a short massage to his hands before he began to work.

开工前，店主给自己的手做了个短暂的按摩。

There were numerous parts on the desk, in fact innumerable.

桌上零件繁多，实际上数不胜数。

He had to multiply the numbers in his head.

他得在脑子里把这些数字相乘。

To accomplish a single clock, he had to achieve three things at once: fulfill the order, keep the work fulfilling, and never leave a full drawer half full.

要完成一只钟，他得同时做到三件事：交付订单、让这份工作保持成就感、绝不让一个满抽屉只剩半满。

An empty shelf would fill again by morning.

空架子到早上又会装满。

He spoke about reality only in terms of the work in front of him, and he did not realize that his own realm was shrinking.

他谈起现实，只以眼前这件活计为准；他没意识到自己的天地正在缩小。

Over the years he had accumulated a great deal, and he could calculate the value of each piece.

这些年他积累了很多，他能算出每一件的价值。

He marked the calendar by the clocks he finished.

他用做完的钟来标记日历。

For now he could only assume the best, and resume work tomorrow.

眼下他只能往好处想，明天再继续。

He did not presume to know how it would end.

他不敢妄自揣测结局如何。
