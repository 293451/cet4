The Last Story at Gull's Point海鸥角的最后一个故事

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Every spring, the same thing occurs on the mud beside the harbour of Gull's Point: deep marks recur in exactly the same place.

每年春天，海鸥角港口旁的泥滩上都会发生同一件事：同一个地方会一再出现深深的印痕。

Nobody can trace them.

谁也追查不出它们的来历。

A boy named Tom once followed the trail for a mile and tried to track the animal that left it, but the tide washed every track away.

一个叫汤姆的男孩曾沿着那道踪迹走了一英里，想追踪留下它的那只动物，可潮水把每个足迹都冲掉了。

"We must curb such wild stories," said the mayor.

“我们得管住这些胡编的故事，”镇长说。

The stories came back anyway.

可故事还是照样回来。

Tom's little sister Amy liked to splash through the shallow water.

汤姆的小妹妹艾米喜欢在浅水里哗啦哗啦地蹚水。

Her face would flush with joy, and she would blush when the other children turned to look.

她高兴得满脸通红，别的孩子一看她，她就脸红。

Nearly everyone in the town shared one surname, namely Lin.

镇上几乎所有人都同一个姓，也就是姓林。

In the old house by the water, an infant slept, adolescents idled on the stone steps, and a youngster ran along the shore with a dog.

水边的老屋里，一个婴儿在睡觉，几个青少年在石阶上闲坐着，一个少年带着狗沿着岸边跑。

Only the elder of the family, Grandmother Lin, still worked hard.

只有家里的长辈林奶奶还在辛苦地干活。

Years before, her two sons had emigrated to Canada, and a new teacher from the north had immigrated to the town.

多年前，她的两个儿子移居去了加拿大，而一位从北方来的新教师移居到了这个镇上。

Every autumn, wild birds migrated over the harbour.

每年秋天，野鸟都会从港口上空迁徙而过。

Tonight the town held its storytelling contest.

今晚，镇上要举办讲故事比赛。

The setting was simple: a lamp, a carpet on the floor, and the moon above the water.

场地很简陋：一盏灯、地上一块地毯，还有水面上方的月亮。

In that context, the children who usually detested reading stood up and spoke.

在这样的背景下，那些平时讨厌读书的孩子都站起来开了口。

When the mayor tried to cancel the contest, the parents protested loudly.

镇长想取消比赛时，家长们大声抗议。

Grandmother Lin told the last story, about a cargo ship, a long career at sea, and the scar on her hand.

林奶奶讲了最后一个故事，关于一艘货船、一段漫长的海上生涯，和她手上的那道疤。

Everyone forgot their appetite for supper and listened.

大家都忘了吃晚饭的胃口，只顾听着。
