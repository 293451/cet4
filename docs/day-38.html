The Rain That Came to Sand Cove雨落在沙湾

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The village of Sand Cove stood on a barren coast, where the soil was shallow and the rain had been deficient for nine weeks.

沙湾村坐落在一段贫瘠的海岸上，那里的土层很浅，雨水已经缺了九个星期。

The wells were low, and there was barely sufficient water for the sheep.

井水很浅，勉强够羊喝。

Every morning the men hauled barrels and buckets up the hill.

每天早上，男人们把一桶桶水拖上山。

Two boys had to drag a handcart behind them, and by noon they could hardly lift a full bucket onto the truck.

两个男孩还得在后面拽一辆手推车，到中午，他们几乎抬不动满满一桶水上车。

At noon they rested in the shade of a grey rock.

正午，他们就在一块灰石头下的阴凉里歇脚。

Down at the harbour, the market opened at six.

港口边的集市六点开张。

An old woman bargained over the price of fish, and in addition to the fish she asked for free salt.

一位老太太为鱼价讨价还价，除了鱼，她还要免费的盐。

The seller laughed, which embarrassed her, and she turned her cheek away.

卖鱼的人笑了，这让她有点尴尬，便把脸侧了过去。

That August the village held a ceremony to celebrate the rain.

那年八月，村里办了一场典礼来庆祝这场雨。

Before the first cloud appeared, the children began to cheer.

第一片云还没出现，孩子们就开始欢呼起来。

A young teacher raised both arms and seemed to elevate the whole crowd, and the pleasure on every face was easy to see.

一位年轻老师举起双臂，好像把整个人群都托了起来，每个人脸上的喜悦都看得见。

When the storm finally came, the wind began to roar.

暴风雨终于来时，风开始咆哮。

It was no silly game.

这可不是什么犯傻的游戏。

The fishermen ran for shelter behind the harbour wall, which had protected boats for two hundred years.

渔民们跑到港墙后面躲避，那堵墙替船只挡了两百年的风雨。

Someone brought fuel for the lamps, and others buried the fish boxes in wet sand.

有人拿来了灯油，还有人把鱼箱埋进湿沙里。

To amuse the children, a young man who had shaved his head put on an artificial dragon head and ran down the street.

为了让孩子们开心，一个刚剃了头的年轻人套上一个人造的龙头，沿街跑了起来。

It could not really roar, and the fishermen were proficient at mending nets but not at making toys, so they knew it looked silly.

它其实不会吼，而这些渔民补网很在行、做玩具却不在行，所以他们知道它看着挺傻。

Still, it was efficient enough to make the whole village laugh.

可它还是够管用的，让全村人都笑了。
