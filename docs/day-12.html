课文

Grandpa's old fishing boat had been sitting under a grey blanket in the yard for years.

爷爷那艘旧渔船盖着一块灰色毯子，在院子里放了好多年。

The cabin was vacant, and the floor was so dusty that I had to vacuum it before I could step inside.

船舱是空的，地板上积满灰尘，我得先用吸尘器吸一遍才敢踏进去。

There was a lot to discard: bent hooks, torn nets, and three bags of trash.

要丢掉的东西太多了：弯了的鱼钩、破渔网，还有三袋垃圾。

The hull still carried a long crack from a collision with the dock, and a second one from a clash with a steel buoy.

船身上还留着一道长长的裂缝，是当年和码头撞出来的；另一道是和钢制浮标撞出来的。

Grandpa once watched a small boat crash into the harbour wall.

爷爷曾经眼睁睁看着一条小船撞上港口的墙。

The wreck lay on the beach all winter, and every storm would smash it a little smaller.

那条船的残骸在沙滩上躺了一整个冬天，每场暴风雨都把它砸得更碎一点。

When he told that story, he would suddenly dash outside to check his own ropes.

他讲这个故事的时候，总会突然冲出去检查自己的缆绳。

I had no cash to repair the boat, and I was ashamed to ask my family for money.

我没有现金修船，也不好意思向家里要钱。

Still, I wanted to advance the work, so I asked my uncle to approve a small loan.

不过我还是想把活儿往前推进，就去请叔叔批准一小笔借款。

He wanted proof that she could still sail, and said a good coat of paint would enhance her value, enlarge her market, and enrich the whole harbour.

他要看到这条船还能航行的证据，还说好好刷一层漆能提高她的身价、扩大她的市场，也能让整个港口受益。

So I learned to fasten every rope and pull it tight.

于是我学着把每根缆绳都系牢、拉紧。

I made the deck watertight and checked that nothing was loose.

我把甲板做成防水的，还检查了有没有哪里松动。

One last thing had to be undone.

还有最后一件事需要解开。

A narrow closet enclosed the space under the bench, held shut by a hook and a broken lock.

长凳下面是窄窄一个壁橱，被一个钩子和一把坏锁扣着。

At first I did not disclose the find to anyone.

起初我没有把发现告诉任何人。

Inside, in a sealed tin, lay a photograph and a letter my grandfather had tried to conceal for forty years.

里面有个封好的铁盒，盒里是一张照片，还有一封爷爷藏了四十年的信。
