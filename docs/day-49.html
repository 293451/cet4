The Bowl and the Bridge碗与桥

0:00 / 0:00

课文

After the flood, the old stone bridge at Little Ford began to disintegrate.

洪水过后，小渡口那座老石桥开始解体。

The cement between the stones had turned to sand, and no rail was intact.

石头之间的水泥已经变成了沙，没有一段栏杆是完好的。

One beam was still attached to the bank, clinging to a rock.

有一根横梁还连在河岸上，紧紧抓着一块石头。

An, who was twelve, put his hand into the crack and felt something tangible.

十二岁的阿安把手伸进裂缝里，摸到一样实实在在的东西。

It was a piece of a blue bowl, with a bit of old glue sticking to it.

那是一块蓝碗的碎片，上面还粘着一点旧胶水。

He had no clue whose bowl it was; no name was on it.

他完全不知道这只碗是谁的，上面也没有名字。

His grandmother came and held it in both hands.

奶奶走过来，用两只手捧住了它。

"That is a cue I never wanted," she said. "My mother gave it to me."

“这是我最不想要的提示，”她说，“这是我妈给我的。”

Her voice was bitter, and her eyes were full of sorrow.

她的声音发苦，眼里满是悲伤。

For three days she sat in misery and groaned at small sounds.

整整三天，她坐在痛苦里，听见一点小声音就哼哼。

The loss was painful, and An watched her despair grow.

这份失去是痛的，阿安看着她的绝望一点点涨起来。

He compared the broken bowl with the bridge and decided to fix both.

他把破碗和破桥放在一起比较，决定两个都修。

"You cannot," said the neighbours. "Your desperation will hurt you."

“你修不好的，”邻居们说，“你这样拼命会伤到自己。”

But he did not work desperately; he worked slowly.

可他并不是在绝望地干，他干得很慢。

He tried to calm his grandmother and asked her to relax.

他试着让奶奶平静下来，请她歇一歇、看着他做。

She said the work gave her back her nerve.

她说，这活把她那点精气神又还给她了。

He glued the pieces together and filled the gaps with cement.

他把碎片粘起来，用新的水泥补上缺口。

The village recommended a mason, who commended his patience.

村里推荐了一位泥瓦匠，这位师傅夸他有耐心。

For two weeks trucks had to crawl along the river road.

有两个星期，卡车只能沿着河边那条路慢慢爬。

Then the bridge was open, and the first car accelerated.

然后桥通了，第一辆车加了速。

"Do not exaggerate," said the mason. "It is a small bridge."

“别夸张，”泥瓦匠说，“这只是座小桥。”

At the far side, drivers had to decelerate to make the turn.

到了对岸，司机都得再减一次速才能转过弯。

The bowl stood on the shelf, in contact with the morning light.

那只碗摆在架子上，和清晨的光贴在一起。

An kept in contact with the mason, and they mended two bridges.

阿安一直和泥瓦匠保持着联系，他们一共修好了两座桥。
