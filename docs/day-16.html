The Museum Above the Harbour海港上的博物馆

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Omar once served on a submarine, and he can still recall the cold, dark water of the deep sea.

奥马尔曾在潜艇上服役，他至今还能想起深海那冰冷黑暗的海水。

When the boat had to submerge, the whole crew held their breath; minutes later it would emerge again into the light.

当潜艇必须下潜时，全船人都屏住呼吸；几分钟后，它又会浮出水面，重见天光。

One night a pipe broke, and the captain called it an emergency.

一天夜里，一根管子爆了，船长说这是紧急情况。

That was the year Omar began to love marine life, and he wrote it all in a small memo book.

就是那一年，奥马尔开始爱上海洋生物，并把一切都写进一本小备忘录里。

Today he runs a tiny museum on the coast, and every exhibit tells a story.

如今他在海边经营一座小小的博物馆，每一件展品都有一段故事。

He likes to display the models in the same order as that old voyage.

他喜欢照那次航行的顺序来陈列模型。

When the door opens, a breeze from the sea comes in.

门一开，海上的微风就吹了进来。

In winter the bay will freeze, and visitors squeeze past one another.

冬天海湾会结冰，游客们只能挤着互相错身。

Children gaze at the model boat.

孩子们会盯着那艘模型船看。

Most adults only glance at it, and a few catch a glimpse of the captain's logbook.

大多数大人只是瞥一眼，少数人能瞄到船长的旧航海日志。

One man stared at a photograph, and then he glared at his phone.

有个人盯着一张照片看了一会儿，随后又瞪着自己的手机。

A note on the wall reminded Omar of his promise: this small monument must never fade.

墙上的一张便条提醒奥马尔他自己许下的诺言：这座小小的纪念碑绝不能被淡忘。

Last spring a young man asked for work, so Omar employed him.

去年春天，一个年轻人来求职，奥马尔雇用了他。

It was the boy's first employment, and he was proud of it.

这是男孩的第一份工作，他为此很得意。

But he was lazy, and in the end Omar had to fire him.

但他很懒，最后奥马尔不得不解雇他。

The boy began to plot something unkind, and he said he would take a shot at the museum in the local paper.

男孩于是开始策划一件不太善意的事，他说要在本地报纸上给博物馆来一枪。

The hall is flat and old, and every Saturday Omar would inflate a rubber whale.

展厅又平又旧，每个星期六奥马尔都会吹一只橡皮鲸鱼。

In autumn the fog is dense, and water condenses on the cold glass of the cases.

秋天，雾很浓，水汽在冰凉的玻璃柜面上凝成水珠。

His old wool sweater tends to shrink, and the ink on the labels has faded.

他的旧羊毛衫容易缩水，标签上的墨水也褪了色。
