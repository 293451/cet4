The Cartoon in the Fishing Town渔村里的动画片

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Last summer a film crew came to our town to make a cartoon about the old fishermen.

去年夏天，一个摄制组来到我们镇上，要拍一部关于老渔民的动画片。

The director wanted to portray their life honestly.

导演想诚实地描绘他们的生活。

Every morning a small band played on the beach, and a gang of children ran after the camera.

每天早上都有一支小乐队在海滩上演奏，一帮孩子追着摄像机跑。

Even the gulls flocked to the boats.

连海鸥都成群地飞向渔船。

The story was a battle against a beast from the hills.

故事讲的是与山里一头野兽的战役。

The fishermen had to combat it.

渔民们必须与它搏斗。

They beat drums and hit the water with long poles.

他们敲鼓，用长竿打水。

The young hero never used a hammer or a punch.

年轻的男主角从没用过锤子，也没挥过拳头。

He only tied a bandage around the animal's leg, and the beast walked away.

他只是把一条绷带缠在野兽的腿上，那头野兽就走开了。

In July the town is a quiet resort, and the director was a classic city man from a middle-class family.

七月里小镇是个安静的度假胜地，而导演是个典型的中产阶级出身的城里人。

He liked to classify people by their jobs.

他喜欢按职业给人分类。

With money from the city he also wanted to upgrade the old cinema.

带着城里来的钱，他还想把老电影院升级一下。

One evening there was a long debate, almost a dispute, about the ending.

一天晚上，关于结局有一场漫长的争论，几乎算得上争吵。

A young editor gave a hint.

一位年轻剪辑师给了个暗示。

"Do not just say it," he said.

“别只是说出来，”他说。

"Demonstrate it. Illustrate it with a short demo."

“要演示出来。用一个短短的样品展示出来。”

His words seemed to imply a warning, though the warning was only implicit.

他话里似乎暗示着一个警告，尽管那警告只是含蓄的。

He was never explicit.

他从不明说。

"Every picture must convey one feeling," he added.

“每一幅画面都必须传达一种情绪，”他补充道。

A survey of the town showed that most people wanted the happy ending.

镇上的一项调查显示，大多数人想要那个圆满结局。

So the crew decided to invest the rest of the money in a second episode.

于是剧组决定把剩下的钱投进第二集。
