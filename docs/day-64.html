The Old Hangar at Fairview Field费尔维尤机场的旧机库

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Fairview Field is a small airport at the edge of a wide plain.

费尔维尤机场很小，坐落在广阔平原的边缘。

Nora repairs aircraft in an old wooden hangar there.

诺拉在那儿一座旧木机库里修飞机。

She learned her craft from an uncle who spent forty years in aviation.

她的手艺是跟舅舅学的，那位舅舅在航空业干了四十年。

Every quarter she walks the same line of planes and checks every flight.

每个季度她都沿着同一排飞机走一遭，检查每一个航班。

In the morning a jet from a big airline rolls up to the terminal.

早晨，一架大型航空公司的喷气式飞机缓缓滑到航站楼前。

At noon the take-off of a helicopter shakes the grass by the runway.

中午，一架直升机的起飞震得跑道边的草直晃。

A poster of The Terminator hangs above her tools.

她的工具上方贴着《终结者》的海报。

A grey pigeon lives under the roof and watches her work.

一只灰鸽子住在屋顶下，看着她干活。

One cold Tuesday a dentist from the coast arrived with a heavy black bag.

一个寒冷的星期二，一位从海边来的牙医拎着一只沉甸甸的黑包到了。

An hour later a second traveler came in with an identical bag.

一小时后，第二位旅客进来，拎着一只一模一样的包。

Nora had to identify which bag belonged to whom.

诺拉必须辨认出哪只包是谁的。

Both were old and worn, with nothing identifiable on them.

两只包都又旧又破，上面没有任何可辨认的标记。

The people of the town worship this little field.

镇上的人崇拜这一小片机场。

On Sundays they stand by the fence like a herd of sheep.

星期天他们就站在围栏边，像一群羊。

Nora is inclined to love it just as much.

诺拉也差不多一样爱它。

But last winter the airline declined to keep its morning flight.

可去年冬天，那家航空公司谢绝了继续开早班航班。

The manager asked her to list every shortcoming of the field.

经理让她把这座机场的每一个缺点都列出来。

The biggest disadvantage was the distance from the city.

最大的劣势是它离城里太远。

Another drawback was the old control tower.

另一个缺点是一座老控制塔。

Rain could weaken the radio signal and the lights.

雨水会削弱无线电信号和跑道上的灯。

Every setback made the little airport look smaller.

每一次挫折都让这个小机场显得更小。

The only real obstacle was money.

唯一真正的障碍是钱。

Snow and wind would hinder repair work in winter.

雪和风会妨碍冬天的一切维修。

The small staff had lived with hardship: low pay, cold rooms, long hours.

多年来，这几个员工一直过着苦日子：工资低、屋子冷、工时长得没边。

So Nora and two helpers wired the control room themselves in six days.

于是诺拉和两个帮手自己动手，六天就给控制室接好了电线。

In April the airline agreed to stay, and the morning jet still comes.

四月，那家航空公司同意留下来，早晨的喷气机照旧飞来。
