The Boy Who Climbed the Ridge爬上山脊的男孩

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Every summer, the boy went down to the seaside.

每年夏天，男孩都会去海边。

He would sail a small boat close to the shore, then land on a quiet island.

他会驾一条小船靠近海岸，然后登上一座安静的小岛。

He liked to sit on a bench on the beach and watch the waves.

他喜欢坐在沙滩的长椅上，看海浪。

Inland, the hills rose behind the town.

在内陆方向，群山从小镇背后隆起。

One day he decided to climb them.

有一天，他决定去爬那些山。

He took a rope and tied a firm bond around his waist, and used it to bind the pack to his back.

他拿了一根绳子，在腰间系成一个牢固的结，用它把背包绑在背上。

The path began to bend, then to twist around the rocks.

小路开始弯折，接着绕着岩石盘旋。

The colours of the fields seemed to blend together and mix into one, and the air was a mixture of pine and salt.

田野的颜色似乎交融在一起、混成一片，空气里是松树和盐的混合气味。

He stopped to stir the dust with his boot.

他停下来，用靴子搅了搅脚下的尘土。

Far above, a fountain of white water fell from a high mountain.

在高处，一道白色的水柱从高山上倾泻而下。

The view was profound, and it shook the foundation of everything he thought he knew.

眼前的景象意味深长，动摇了他此前所知一切的根基。

Slowly, he began to mount the last slope.

他慢慢地开始爬上最后一道坡。

Birds seemed to soar over the ridge.

鸟儿似乎在山脊上方翱翔。

He could see the full amount of the valley below him.

他能看到下方山谷的全部。

To ascend further was hard, but he kept going.

再往上攀登很艰难，但他继续往前走。

His grandfather had been a descendant of the first settlers here, and he was the old man's only offspring.

他的祖父是这里最早一批定居者的后裔，而他是老人唯一的孩子。

Their family was ancient, and every ancestor had walked this path.

他们的家族很古老，每一位祖先都走过这条路。

To proceed was a kind of duty.

继续前行，像是一种责任。

At the top, a small procession of other walkers joined him, and together they watched the sun go down.

在山顶，一小队其他登山者加入了进来，他们一起看着太阳落下。
