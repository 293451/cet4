The Cutter in the Mountain Shed山间棚屋里的打磨人

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Old Chen had a beard as white as salt and a small shed behind his house.

老陈的胡子白得像盐，屋后有一间小小的棚屋。

Every morning he would grind a rough stone against the wheel and polish it until it shone.

每天早上，他都要把一块粗石贴到砂轮上磨，一直抛光到发亮。

He had lived in this region for forty years and knew its rocks well.

他在这个地区住了四十年，对这里的石头了如指掌。

He explored the old mine alone, but he was never reckless; he never went in without a lamp.

他喜欢一个人去探那座老矿，但他从不莽撞；没有灯，他绝不进去。

Others came to exploit the mountain for quick money, and he did not.

别人是来开采这座山、赚快钱的，他不是。

One spring the mine had to be opened again, and workers set a small blast at the far end.

有一年春天，矿又要重新打开，工人们在最里头放了一小炮。

The burst of sound came, rock exploded outward, and the shock could tear a coat open.

爆响传来，岩石向外炸开，那股冲击力能把一件外套撕开。

In the broken wall he found a treasure: a clear crystal, the size of a plum.

在裂开的岩壁里，他找到了一件宝贝：一块李子大小的透明水晶。

It was precious enough to make his hands shake.

它珍贵得让他的手都抖了起来。

He could not estimate its worth, only give an approximate price, and even that was a guess.

他估不出它的价值，只能给一个大概的价钱，而那也只是猜的。

He reckoned it might pay for a new roof, but selling it in a hurry would devalue it.

他估算这够换一个新屋顶，但急着卖出去只会让它贬值。

The ultimate price, he felt, was not money.

他觉得，最终的价格不在钱上。

This crystal had its origin deep in the mountain; such stones originate in heat and pressure.

这块水晶的源头在山的深处；这类石头是在高温和压力中生成的。

From the same rock he had once derived a small blue one, and the mine never deprived him of work.

同一块岩石里，他还曾开出过一小块蓝色的，而这座矿从没让他没活干。

Few things thrive in this hard region, yet he had thrived and meant to strive.

在这片贫瘠的地方，很少有东西能长得兴旺，可他却兴旺起来了，还打算一直奋斗下去。

His wife had been gone for years.

他的妻子已经走了很多年。

Their life had been intimate and quiet, and he kept a record of every stone he cut.

他们的日子曾经亲密而安静，他把自己切过的每一块石头都记在本子上。

He did not regard the crystal as property but as a guest.

他不把这块水晶当成财产，而当成一位客人。

His work did not accord with fashion, and he did not care.

他的手艺跟时髦并不合拍，他也不在乎。
