Reading Night at Raven Hill雷文山的阅读之夜

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Lily stood on a hill above the town and watched a golden eagle turn over the valley.

莉莉站在小镇上方的一座小丘上，看着一只金雕在山谷上空盘旋。

Its shape in the pale sky was sharp; nothing escaped its eyes.

它在淡色天空里的身形锐利；什么也逃不过它的眼睛。

Below her, the library was busy with its yearly reading night, when people talked about literature.

在她下方，图书馆正忙着筹备一年一度的阅读之夜，那一晚人们谈论的是文学。

Lily had entered the contest with a piece about her grandmother's kitchen, quoting three lines from a famous writer.

莉莉带着一篇写外婆厨房的文章参加了比赛，里面引用了某位名作家的三行文字。

Her teacher said the piece could exemplify what a young reader feels; her literacy had grown that year.

她的老师说，这篇东西足以例证一个年轻读者的感受；她那一年读写能力长进不少。

"Do not read it literally," the teacher had warned.

“不要照着字面去读，”老师事先警告过她。

This year the judge was a demanding editor, and the rules required everyone to read aloud.

今年的评委是一位要求很高的编辑，规则要求每个人都要大声朗读。

A boy made a request to read first; Lily did not mind.

一个男孩请求第一个读；莉莉并不介意。

She inquired about the time limit; the editor said he would suspend the clock for nobody.

她询问时间限制；编辑说不会为任何人暂停计时。

There was a great demand for seats, and everyone hoped the rain would cease before nine.

座位需求很大，大家都盼着雨能在九点前停。

It did not cease, and the buses had to halt.

雨没有停，公交车只好停下。

A girl beside her tried to discourage her; her ticket had expired.

旁边一个女孩想让她气馁；她说自己的票已经过期了。

Lily would not let that dispirit her.

莉莉不会让这件事令她沮丧。

She kept her spirit quiet; her grandmother, eager to hear the piece, sat near the front.

她让自己的精神保持安静；而她渴望听这篇文章的外婆，坐在靠前的位置。

Her only desire was to finish without a pause.

她唯一的愿望就是中间不停顿地念完。

Her grandmother had encouraged her to write daily, and an old writer inspired her to keep a notebook.

外婆一直鼓励她每天写作，还有一位老作家激励她坚持记笔记。

That writer became her inspiration, someone she hoped to aspire to be.

那位作家成了她的灵感，成了她渴望成为的那种人。

At the end, the editor stood up.

最后，编辑站了起来。

"To want to be read," he said, "is the first quest of a writer."

“想要被读到，”他说，“是每一位作家的第一个追求。”

Lily did not win, but she acquired a lesson.

莉莉没有获奖，但她得到了一堂课。

She decided to subscribe to a literary magazine, and the editor inscribed her name in the first copy.

她决定订阅一本文学杂志，编辑在头一本的里面题上了她的名字。
