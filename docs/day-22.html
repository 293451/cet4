The Council of Green County格林县议会的一次表决

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The council of Green County met on a rainy Tuesday to propose a new plan for the railway.

一个下雨的周二，格林县议会开会，要提一份关于铁路的新方案。

The mayor stood up and imposed her purpose on the room: bring the city train into the countryside.

县长站起来，把自己的目的强加到整个会场：让城里的火车开进乡村。

She proposed that the county deposit a large sum and compose a transport committee.

她提议由县里存下一大笔钱，再组建一个交通委员会。

Every component of the plan was written down, and the composition ran to ten pages.

方案的每一个组成部分都写了下来，整篇稿子足足有十页。

Mr. Hale was her strongest opponent.

黑尔先生是她最强劲的对手。

He took the opposite position at once and said he would oppose the plan to the end.

他立刻站到了相反的立场上，说他会反对这个方案到底。

He even asked the council to depose the mayor before the vote.

他甚至要求议会在表决前罢免县长。

A retired teacher was asked to counsel both sides.

大家请了一位退休教师来为双方提供咨询。

She said the council should not dispose of such a plan so quickly, and her words made the mood positive.

她说，议会不该这么快就把这样一个方案丢掉，她的话让气氛变得积极起来。

The question underneath was simple.

底下的问题其实很简单。

Green County was rural: fields, small roads and a few farms.

格林县是乡村：田野、小路，还有几个农场。

The nearest urban area was forty miles away.

最近的城区在四十英里之外。

Families in the suburb drove downtown every morning.

郊区的人家每天早上开车进市区。

The metropolitan train stopped at the county line and went no further.

大都市的火车停在县界，再不肯往前开。

Its citizens wanted modern civilization, and they felt a civil right to travel.

这里的公民想要现代文明，觉得自己有坐车的公民权利。

The country, they argued, should not forget small places.

他们说，这个国家不该忘记小地方。

On the surface the plan looked expensive, and the preface of the report said so.

从表面上看，这个方案很贵，报告的前言也是这么写的。

But the mayor believed the line would surpass every old record, and she was ready to sail without a compass.

但县长相信这条线路会超过以往任何记录，她准备不靠指南针就扬帆出海。

When the vote came, there was a surge of hands in the air: twenty to seven.

表决时，一片手臂刷地举了起来：二十票对七票。
