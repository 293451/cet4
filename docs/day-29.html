The Palace on Campus校园里的那座宫殿

0:00 / 0:00

课文

In September, the new academic year brings a thousand new students to Jiangcheng Academy.

九月，新的学年把一千名新学生带进江城学院。

Every freshman carries a heavy bag and a light heart.

每个新生都背着一个沉书包，揣着一颗轻快的心。

Every undergraduate carries a heavy schedule too.

每个本科生的课表也一样沉。

The dormitory doors open at eight, and the campus is loud all day.

宿舍八点开门，校园里整天都吵吵嚷嚷。

On the first morning, a tall boy named Han stood in front of the old building called the Palace.

第一天早上，一个叫韩的高个子男生站在那栋叫“宫殿”的老楼前。

Long ago, people say, a prince and a princess once lived there.

据说很久以前，一位王子和一位公主曾住在这里。

Now it holds the offices of the principal and the dean.

如今这里是校长和院长的办公室。

The principal spoke about one principle: work first, play later.

校长只讲了一条原则：先做事，后玩耍。

Han's discipline is computer science, but he also loves art.

韩的专业是计算机，但他也喜欢艺术。

The dean of the faculty told him to read one journal a week.

院长让他每周读一本期刊。

Every Friday there is a seminar, where the experts on the faculty answer questions.

每周五有一场研讨会，系里的专家们在那里回答问题。

Students also talk on the online forum.

学生们也在网上论坛里聊天。

A senior scholar there helped him interpret a difficult paper.

那里一位资深的学者帮他解释了一篇难懂的论文。

Slowly he learned how to interact with teachers and how to ask good questions.

慢慢地，他学会了怎么和老师互动，怎么提出好问题。

At the interval of the meeting, he met a girl who was the school champion at running.

会议中场休息时，他遇见了一个女生，她是校里的跑步冠军。

She was leading a campaign to recycle paper on campus.

她正在发起一场校园纸张回收活动。

In the afternoon Han had an interview for a scholarship.

下午，韩参加了一场奖学金的面试。

His family's tuition was high, and the scholarship could cover half of it.

他家要交的学费很高，而奖学金能抵掉一半。

The teachers did not intervene; they only listened.

老师们并没有干预，只是听着。

The interview was hard, but he stayed calm.

面试很难，但他一直很镇定。

In the winter, his whole class went to a training camp in the hills.

那年冬天，他整个班去山里的一处训练营地。

Nobody could interfere with that week, and nobody wanted to.

没人能打扰那一周，也没人想去打扰。

02 interfere /Intər'fIr/ v. 干涉，阻碍；弄坏

根inter(中间) + fere(撞) → 插到中间乱撞 → 干涉，阻碍
