A Baker's Glory in Maple Hollow枫谷面包房的荣耀

0:00 / 0:00

课文

In Maple Hollow, the fields were full of grain by late June, and the wheat stood tall and gold.

在枫谷，六月底田野里满是谷物，小麦高高地立着，一片金黄。

At sunrise the whole field would glitter, and the tops of the grass would glow.

日出时整片田野都在闪光，草尖上泛着光。

Every drop of dew would sparkle, and above them the last stars would still twinkle.

每一滴露水都在闪耀，它们上方还有最后几颗星星在眨眼。

Mara ran the only bakery in the village.

玛拉在村里开着唯一一家面包房。

She baked her bread with local wheat flour and a little honey.

她用本地小麦磨的面粉和一点蜂蜜烤面包。

People said her bread had a flavour no city shop could copy, and it was agreeable to everyone.

人们说她的面包有一种城里店铺学不来的味道，而且人人都觉得它可口。

Her mother had taught her to work with grace.

母亲教过她，干活要带着优雅。

"Never bring disgrace to this shop," she used to say.

“永远别给这家店丢脸，”她过去常这样说。

Mara's hands were elegant, and her quiet charm won the village.

玛拉的手灵巧优雅，她那份安静的魅力赢得了全村人。

To her, baking was a matter of honor, and the smell of it in the street was a kind of glory.

对她来说，烤面包关乎荣誉，街上飘着的那股香味就是一种荣耀。

That autumn, the county held a baking fair.

那年秋天，县里办了一场烘焙评比。

The judges were prominent figures: an eminent cook and a famous food writer.

评委都是响当当的人物：一位有名的厨师，还有一位知名的美食作家。

One of them said her work was extraordinary, and her crust was outstanding.

其中一位说她的手艺非凡，面包皮更是出色。

A young commentator wrote a long remark about her quiet manner, and his comment appeared in the paper the next day.

一位年轻的评论员写了一大段关于她安静气质的评语，他的评论第二天就登了报。

He put a mark in his notebook: "ordinary bread, extraordinary hands."

他在笔记本上做了一个标记：“普通的面包，非凡的双手。”

Everyone in the village could see her progress.

村里每个人都能看到她的进步。

Mara was chosen as the delegate to the small congress of bakers, which met in the old parliament hall twice a year.

玛拉被选为面包师小型代表大会的代表，这个大会每年在老议会大厅开两次。

At the meeting one baker became aggressive and insulted the others.

会上有一位面包师变得很凶，辱骂了别人。

He kept digressing until the chairman stopped him.

他一直跑题，直到主席制止了他。

In the end the consensus was clear, and she took the top prize.

最后共识很清楚：她拿下了头奖。

She received the favour of the whole town.

她得到了全镇人的喜爱。
