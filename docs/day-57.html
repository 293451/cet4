The Fair on the Water水面上的科学展

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Every October, the science fair of Green Lake is held on an old boat that floats a few metres from the shore.

每年十月，绿湖的科学展都在一条老船上举办，船就浮在离岸几米的水面上。

A heavy anchor holds it steady, so the desks never move.

一个沉重的锚把它稳住，所以桌子从来不会移动。

At the front of the boat, two students set up a model of the atom, a glass tube full of electrons, and a small board that makes electricity from sunlight.

船头，两个学生搭起一个原子模型、一根装满电子的玻璃管，还有一块用阳光发电的小板子。

Beside them hangs a poster about nuclear energy and a picture of an astronaut fixing a satellite in space.

他们旁边挂着一张关于核能的招贴画，和一张宇航员在太空修理卫星的照片。

Mr. Lin, their teacher, sits below the deck and edits the videos they made.

他们的老师林先生坐在甲板下面，剪辑他们拍的视频。

He keeps every version in one file, writes a short profile of each student, and lists the main features of their work.

他把每个版本都存进同一个文件夹，给每个学生写一段简短的介绍，还列出他们作品的主要特点。

He likes one phrase: "A good question is a whole phase of learning."

他喜欢其中一个短语：“一个好问题就是一整段学习。”

Every project has its own character.

每个项目都有自己的性格。

When the judges come, the students respond at once.

评委走过来时，学生们立刻作出回应。

One respondent fills in a form, and her answers correspond with what the class had planned.

一位受访者填了一张表，她的回答和班里原先的计划完全一致。

The feedback is kind and direct.

反馈既友善又直接。

A local factory sponsors the fair, and the applause that follows is spontaneous and loud.

当地一家工厂赞助了这次展览，随之而来的掌声是自发而响亮的。

By four o'clock, Mr. Lin is weary, and fatigue pulls at his shoulders.

到四点钟，林先生已经疲倦了，疲劳压着他的肩膀。

The whole week has exhausted him.

这一周把他累得筋疲力尽。

Still, he wants to expand the fair next year, and his plan is simple and feasible.

不过，他明年还想把这场展览会办得更大，他的计划简单而可行。

Near the back, a small boy beats a drum, and a white feather floats down from a passing bird.

船尾附近，一个小男孩敲着鼓，一根白羽毛从路过的鸟身上飘落下来。

A girl runs to catch it, slips on the wet deck, and twists her ankle.

一个女孩跑去接它，在湿滑的甲板上滑了一下，扭伤了脚踝。
