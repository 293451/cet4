Day 69 · 12/20 · Unit 10 第 2041-2062 词

The Green Lane Market绿巷小店

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The Green Lane Market opened in 1998 at the end of a narrow lane behind the station.

绿巷小店 1998 年开张，就在车站后面一条窄巷子的尽头。

My aunt ran it for twenty years with her sister, and both of them were left-handed.

我姑姑和她的妹妹一起经营了二十年，她们俩都是左撇子。

She could open a tin, weigh rice and write a bill with one hand while she talked to a customer.

她能一只手开罐头、称米、写账单，同时还能跟顾客说话。

Beforehand, she always got ready the night before: she counted the change, cleaned every aisle and checked the shelves.

事先，她总在头天晚上就准备好：数好零钱、擦净每一条过道、检查货架。

She could not comprehend why people drove to the new so-called super store on the ring road.

她无法理解，为什么人们要开车去环路那家所谓的超级大卖场。

She never tried to satisfy everyone, and she never answered a critic.

她从没想过要让每个人都满意，也从不去回应批评她的人。

Criticism, she said, is free advice, and you do not have to take it.

她说，批评是免费的建议，但你不一定非得接受。

The former manager of the chain came to see the shop one afternoon.

那家连锁店的前任经理有天下午来看这间店。

He said his company made a billion a year and that the town could not support two shops.

他说他的公司一年赚十亿，而这个镇养不起两家店。

Only one of us can stay, he said, and the choice is critical.

他说，我们俩只能留下一个，而怎么选至关重要。

Later he offered to buy her out; she said no.

后来他提出把她买下来，她说了不。

Meanwhile, the small shop stayed open, whereas the super store closed within a year.

与此同时，小店照常开着，而那家大卖场不到一年就关了门。

There was no other reason for this, and it did not mean that my aunt was clever.

这没有别的原因，也并不意味着我姑姑有多聪明。

Lower prices were only a means, never the aim.

低价只是一种手段，从来不是目的。

She knew the name of every customer, thereby turning strangers into friends.

她叫得出每个顾客的名字，从而把陌生人变成了朋友。

Moreover, she knew exactly what to do when something went wrong.

而且，一旦出了什么问题，她很清楚该怎么办。

Furthermore, she laughed a lot.

再说，她还特别爱笑。

Nobody else wrote about her, and nobody put her in a chart.

没有别人写过她，也没人把她做进什么图表里。

But the town remembers the lane, the aisle and the woman with the quick left hand.

但全镇人记得那条巷子、那条过道，还有那个左手很快的女人。
