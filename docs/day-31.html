The Architect and the Old Theatre建筑师与老剧院

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Old Mr. Hale was an architect, and he loved the architecture of the little town by the river.

老黑尔先生是位建筑师，他深爱着河边那座小镇的建筑。

For forty years he had watched workers construct bridges, erect towers, and pour the base of every new building.

四十年来，他看着工人们造桥、竖塔，为每一栋新楼打地基。

He always said that a town is only as strong as its infrastructure, and that basic things matter most.

他总说，一座小镇的强弱全看它的基础设施，而基本的东西最重要。

That spring the town asked him to elaborate on a plan for the old theatre.

那年春天，镇里请他为老剧院详细制订一份方案。

On the stage of that theatre, every drama and every comedy of the town had been played.

在那座剧院的舞台上，镇上的每一出戏剧和每一场喜剧都上演过。

"The plan is simple," he said.

“方案很简单，”他说。

"This is not a tragedy, and it is not a ridiculous or absurd idea."

“这不是悲剧，也不是什么可笑或荒谬的想法。”

He opened the index of his notebook and read out the ingredients of his plan.

他翻开笔记本的索引，把方案的各项要素念了出来。

Every element was listed there, and not one factor was forgotten.

每一个要素都列在上面，没有一个因素被漏掉。

His lunch was a paste of beans and a compound of nuts, kept in a small portable box.

他的午餐是豆泥和坚果混合物，装在一个轻便的小盒子里。

Beside the theatre there was a pond, and behind the pond stood a complex of three old houses.

剧院旁边有个池塘，池塘后面立着三栋老房子组成的建筑群。

The drawings seemed to perplex the young mayor.

那些图纸似乎让年轻的镇长困惑不解。

"You make a simple thing complex," the mayor said.

“你把简单的事弄复杂了，”镇长说。

"Complicate it more and the roof will collapse."

“再复杂化下去，屋顶就要塌了。”

Those words did provoke the old man.

这话确实激怒了老人。

"I advocate repair, not replacement," he said, and he struck the table with his whip.

“我拥护修缮，而不是拆掉重建，”他说着，用鞭子敲了敲桌子。

"Sorry, I do not mean to confuse you," he added.

“抱歉，我不是想让你迷惑，”他又说。

"But I will not be blunt about bad work."

“但面对拙劣的工程，我不会说话含糊。”

The mayor laughed and signed.

镇长笑了，签了字。
