The Garden Behind the Science Building理科楼后面的菜园

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Behind the science building at Weston College there is a small garden.

在韦斯顿学院理科楼的后面，有一个小菜园。

The garden is older than the college's master's programme and older than most of the curriculum.

这个菜园比学院的硕士项目还老，比大部分课程都老。

A veteran teacher, Mr. Hale, started it thirty years ago.

三十年前，一位老教师黑尔先生开辟了它。

To a layman, the garden looks like a layer of weeds on a layer of old soil.

在外行眼里，这个菜园就是一层野草盖着一层老土。

To an amateur gardener, it is a careful piece of art.

在业余园艺爱好者眼里，它却是一件用心的艺术品。

Mr. Hale is a pioneer of city farming in this state.

黑尔先生是本州都市耕作的先驱。

He rears chickens, keeps rabbits and cultivates fifty kinds of vegetables; he does not breed anything for money.

他养鸡、养兔，还种五十种蔬菜；他养任何东西都不是为了卖钱。

He uses no chemical spray, and he never tires of telling students why.

他不用任何化学喷剂，总是不厌其烦地告诉学生为什么。

The soil is nourished by leaves, and the plants nourish the people who eat them.

土壤靠落叶滋养，而植物又滋养吃它们的人。

Sophomores, juniors and seniors all take a turn in the garden.

大二、大三和大四的学生都要轮流到菜园里干活。

One sophomore, Lin, is studying sociology.

有个叫林的大二学生，学的是社会学。

A junior is writing about logic, and a senior is drawing the garden with geometry.

一个大三学生在写关于逻辑的文章，一个大四学生用几何来画这个菜园。

There is also a dropout who came back to college at forty.

还有一个辍学者，四十岁又回到了学校。

He wants a bachelor's degree, and then perhaps a master's.

他想拿一个学士学位，然后再读个硕士。

Mr. Hale says the garden is his masterpiece.

黑尔先生说，这个菜园就是他的代表作。

He is not the most sophisticated man, but he is the most intelligent one I know.

他不是最精明世故的人，却是我认识的最聪明的人。

He has a quiet intellect and an intellectual curiosity that never stops.

他有一颗安静的大脑，和一份从不停止的求知的好奇心。

He is articulate too: he can explain a whole season in ten minutes.

他也很会表达：十分钟就能把一整季的事讲清楚。

He acknowledges that he learned most of it from his peers, not from books.

他承认，这些大多是他从同龄人那里学来的，而不是从书本里。

His idea is simple: a team is compatible when each person does what he is good at.

他的想法很简单：当每个人都做自己擅长的事，一个团队就是合得来的。

Every autumn the garden feeds two hundred students.

每年秋天，这个菜园养活两百个学生。
