The Dialect Class方言课

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Professor Lin was a specialist in the dialect of the eastern hills, and his lecture on the first Monday of term was a special event.

林教授是东部山区方言的专家，开学第一个周一的讲座是件特别的事。

The course was not compulsory: students could opt for it or select another.

这门课不是必修的：学生可以选它，也可以选别的。

Forty came, and half of them were optimistic, half pessimistic.

来了四十个人，一半乐观，一半悲观。

"I have one specific aim," he said.

“我只有一个很具体的目标，”他说。

Then he read the specification aloud: three hundred words that exist nowhere else.

然后他把课程说明念了出来：三百个别处不存在的词。

"The speciality of these hills is a word for the sound of rain on stone," he said.

“这片山区的特产，是形容雨打石头那个声音的词，”他说。

Every case was particular, every rule unique, and his handwriting was a little peculiar, even weird.

每个案例都很个别，每条规则都独一无二，他的字也有点古怪，甚至可以说奇怪。

A girl in the front row was bothered by one habit of his.

前排的一个女生被他一个习惯弄得心烦。

He would stop in the middle of a sentence and look at the ceiling.

他会在句子中间停下来，抬头看天花板。

It did annoy her, and she could not ignore it.

这确实让她恼火，可她没法不理它。

But Professor Lin only smiled.

但林教授只是笑了笑。

"Do not neglect the pause," he said.

“别忽视那个停顿，”他说。

"The pause is where the old people put their feeling."

“停顿，是老人家放感情的地方。”

Then he asked the class to collect one word from home.

接着他让全班每人从家里收集一个词。

He supplied boxes and one small supplement, a recorder.

他提供了纸箱，还有一份小小的补充品——一台录音机。

He told each student to embrace the work slowly, and not to be negative or passive.

他让每个学生慢慢地拥抱这项工作，不要消极，也不要被动。

"You will be acquainted with a world you have never heard," he said.

“你们会认识一个从没听过的世界，”他说。

"Thirty years from now, this dialect will be gone."

“三十年后，这种方言就没了。”

"So choose now."

“所以现在就选。”

"Elect one word to carry with you."

“选一个词，带在身上。”
