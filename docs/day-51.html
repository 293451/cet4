The Post Office on the Corner街角的邮局

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The old postman, Mr. Zhao, had worked in the little post office for forty years.

老邮递员赵先生在那间小小的邮局里干了四十年。

He knew the postage of every country by heart.

他凭记忆就知道每个国家的邮费。

Every postcard and every packet was packed, wrapped and sorted by his own hands.

每一张明信片、每一个小包，都是他亲手打包、包裹、分拣的。

"A parcel is not paper," he said.

“一个包裹不是纸，”他说。

"It is somebody's week."

“那是别人的一整个星期。”

He was never absent and always punctual.

他从不缺席，也总是准时。

If a train was late, he would not postpone the delivery; a delay of one hour was a small crime.

如果火车晚点，他也不会推迟投递；在他看来，耽搁一个小时就是小罪过。

He could ascertain at once which letter had been written in a hurry.

他一眼就能查明哪封信是匆匆忙忙写的。

This spring he decided to retire.

今年春天，他决定退休。

The town wanted to present him with a gift, and the mayor would represent the whole town.

镇上想送他一份礼物，仪式由镇长代表全镇来办。

"On behalf of everyone," the mayor said, "we ask for your consent to a small ceremony."

“代表所有人，”镇长说，“我们请求你同意办一场小小的仪式。”

But Mr. Zhao resented the idea of a ceremony.

可赵先生对这种仪式感到气愤。

He did not want to be a representative of anything.

他不想当任何人的代表。

His granddaughter Lin worked at the embassy in the capital, as an assistant to a diplomat.

他的孙女林在首都的大使馆工作，给一位外交官当助理。

She came home with three papers in her bag: a certificate for her language course, a diploma from the night school, and a new driver's license.

她回家时包里带着三张纸：一张语言课程的证书、一张夜校的文凭，还有一本新的驾照。

Next week the school would certify her results.

下周学校会为她的成绩出具证明。

She also had a question to investigate.

她还有一个问题要调查。

How many people in town still wrote letters?

镇上还有多少人写信？

The statistic surprised her.

统计数字让她吃了一惊。

Only nine percent did.

只有百分之九。

Before leaving, she packed a bag, bought a towel for the trip, and climbed the old tower to take photos of the roofs.

走之前，她收拾了行李，为这趟行程买了条毛巾，还爬上那座老塔，拍了几张屋顶的照片。

On the last evening, the mayor lifted a cloth to unveil a stone sign.

最后一个晚上，镇长揭开一块布，为一块石牌揭幕。

For a moment a veil of rain hung over the street.

有那么一会儿，一层雨幕似的水汽挂在街道上方。

Mr. Zhao read the words, said nothing, and took off his cap.

赵先生读了读上面的字，什么也没说，摘下了帽子。
