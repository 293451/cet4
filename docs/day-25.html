The Night the Network Was Hacked网络被黑的那一夜

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Mira worked in a small workshop on the fourth floor.

米拉在四楼的一间小工作室里上班。

Everyone there had a vote; the company was democratic.

那里每个人都有投票权，公司很民主。

Her company was an ally of a larger firm, and both shared one network.

她的公司是一家大公司的盟友，两家共用同一个网络。

One night someone hacked the network and stole everything.

一天夜里，有人侵入网络，把所有东西都偷走了。

The attack did not generate money, but it generated fear.

这次攻击没有产生钱，却产生了恐惧。

A whole generation of young workers had never seen anything like it.

整整一代年轻员工从没见过这样的事。

In general, they had trusted machines too much.

总的来说，他们太相信机器了。

Mira made a strategy.

米拉想出了一个策略。

She began from scratch: she scraped the old files clean, made a catalog of every document, and opened a dialogue with the other team.

她从头开始：把旧文件刮干净，给每份文件编了一份目录，还和另一队人开始了对话。

When the news scattered across the city, she pinched herself and stayed calm.

消息传遍全城时，她掐了自己一把，保持了冷静。

Lawyers said the two firms together constitute one company, and the written constitution of their club was unclear.

律师说，这两家公司合起来构成一家公司，而他们那个俱乐部的章程却写得不清楚。

They had to substitute new rules for old ones and turn the small institute into a real office.

他们只能拿新规矩代替旧的，把这间小小的学会变成真正的办公室。

Some older workers blamed superstition, not code.

一些老员工怪的是迷信，不是代码。

Others blamed religion.

另一些人则怪宗教。

One engineer had been sent into exile years before, and after living abroad even his own street looked exotic.

有位工程师多年前被流放，在国外生活之后，连他自己那条街都显得充满异国情调。

The manager wanted to expel the careless worker, and the rules seemed to compel her to do it.

经理想把那个粗心的员工开除，规则似乎也在逼她这么做。

Mira used all her force to protect him, and in the end nobody in the workforce lost a job.

米拉使出全部力量保护他，最后整个劳动力中没有一个人丢掉工作。

At the workplace everyone felt tired.

在工作场所，每个人都觉得疲惫。

They held a workshop and voted to enforce one simple rule: reinforce every lock with a second key.

他们办了一场研讨会，投票通过一条简单的规矩：每把锁都再加一道保险，加固一遍。
