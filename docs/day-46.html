The Bird Under the Wheel车轮下的小鸟

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Stone Creek is a small town at the foot of a steep slope, and the only road out runs through a tunnel.

石溪镇是个小镇，坐落在一道陡峭山坡的脚下，唯一通向外面的路要穿过一条隧道。

Rainwater runs down the mountain in a stone channel beside the road.

雨水顺着山势，在路边的石砌水渠里流下来。

Mei drives the town's bus and must steer it past the wet stones.

梅开着镇上的公交车，得慢慢地驾驶它绕过那些湿石头。

In hard rain the road turns into a slide of mud and the bus can slip.

雨大的时候，路面就变成一片泥滑梯，车子会打滑。

Last winter she fell and left a dark stain on her blue uniform.

去年冬天她摔了一跤，蓝色制服上留下一块深色的污渍。

Still, she keeps the bus steady, and the old engine is stable.

可她还是把车开得稳稳的，那台老发动机依然稳定。

Her grandfather helped establish the bus line in 1965.

她的爷爷在1965年帮着建立了这条公交线。

Old people say a wicked spirit lives in the tunnel, and in spite of new lights, drivers still pray.

镇上的老人说，隧道里住着一个邪恶的精灵；尽管装了新灯，司机们进洞前还是会祈祷。

"It is not evil," Mei tells them.

“它并不邪恶，”梅对他们说，

"It is only dark and cold."

“它只是又黑又冷。”

One morning a hawk hunted, and its prey ran across the road.

一天早上，一只鹰在捕猎，它的猎物从路上跑了过去。

Mei hit the brake, coffee spilled from her cup, and a bag of rice fell over.

梅一脚踩下刹车，杯里的咖啡洒了出来，一袋大米也翻倒了。

Grain spread across the floor, and some had already spilt by the door.

米粒撒了一地，有些早就从门边溢了出去。

Then she saw a small bird under the wheel.

这时她看见车轮下有一只小鸟。

Its wing was broken, and it was too scared to move.

它的翅膀断了，吓得一动也不敢动。

She did not slap it or shout at it.

她没有拍它，也没有冲它喊。

She washed its wound with a spray of warm water and laid it in a box under a rose bush with a thick stem.

她用一小股温水冲洗它的伤口，把它放进盒子，安置在一丛玫瑰下面——那丛玫瑰的茎很粗。

That evening the town came to the square, and when she put the bird back in its nest, people began to clap.

那天傍晚，镇上的人都来到广场；当她把小鸟放回巢里时，人们开始拍手。

An old woman stopped sweeping to applaud.

一位老太太停下扫帚，为它鼓起掌来。

Lanterns above the square began to sway, and children ran to the swing.

广场上方的灯笼开始摇晃，孩子们跑向秋千。

Above the ridge the moon rose, a pale sphere in the dark.

山脊上方，月亮升起来了，是黑夜里一个苍白的球体。
