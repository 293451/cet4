The Ferry That Would Not Sink不会沉的小渡轮

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The Bellport ferry was a small enterprise that old Mrs. Feng had built into a quiet empire.

贝尔波特的渡轮是老冯太太一手做起来的小企业，后来成了一个不起眼的小帝国。

She was president and chair of the board, and at seventy she still managed the timetable and controlled every cent.

她是总裁兼董事会主席，七十岁了还在安排班次，控制着每一分钱。

Her grandson Lin, a competent young captain, had learned to harness the river and to read the brown water thick with mud.

她的外孙林是个能干的年轻船长，学会了驾驭这条河，也学会了看那浑黄的、掺着泥的水。

Every morning his sister accompanied him to the pier, and the crew of six assisted the passengers onto the boat.

每天早上妹妹陪他到码头，六个人的船员一起扶乘客上船。

That crew consisted of three families, comprising twelve hands in all.

这些船员来自三个家庭，加起来一共十二口人的生计。

A city firm competed for the same route, and in that competition the old ferry, versus the new fast boat, looked slow.

一家城里公司来争同一条航线，在那场竞争里，老渡轮比起新快船，显得很慢。

The family resisted the offer to sell the line and persisted with its own plan.

一家人抵住了卖掉航线的条件，坚持自己的方案。

One spring the flood came, and the lower town was its first victim.

有一年春天洪水来了，地势低的那半个镇子最先遭了殃。

Mrs. Feng made a sacrifice: she sold her own house to repair the pier.

冯太太作出了牺牲：她卖掉了自己的房子来修码头。

The passengers praised her, and the town praised the crew.

乘客称赞她，全镇人也称赞这些船员。

But that same week two men came to the pier with a plan.

可就在那一周，两个男人带着一个打算来到了码头。

They had gambled away their money and made a bet with a smuggler.

他们赌光了钱，和一个走私犯打了赌。

They hid heroin in a cargo box and sold pirated tickets at the gate.

他们在一只货箱里藏了海洛因，还在门口卖盗版船票。

When Lin refused to help them, they kidnapped him and left him in the mud by the river.

林不肯帮他们，他们就把林绑走了，扔在河边的泥里。

He was found at dawn, cold and shaking, and the police caught both men before noon.

天亮时人们找到了他，冻得直抖；中午前警察把两个人都抓了。

In court the older man showed no guilt, but the younger one chose to kneel and weep.

法庭上，年长的那人毫无愧疚，年轻的那个却选择跪下痛哭。

The judge punished them with five years in prison.

法官判他们五年监禁。

Mrs. Feng did not speak to the reporters.

冯太太没有对记者说话。

She only asked whether the ferry could leave at six.

她只问了一句：渡轮能不能六点开。
