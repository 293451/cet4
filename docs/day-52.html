The Weaver at Sandy Bay沙滩湾的织布人

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Grandma Su is eighty-two, and her body has grown feeble and vulnerable to every cold wind.

苏奶奶八十二岁了，身体越来越虚弱，一阵冷风都能伤到她。

Her hands tremble when she lifts the heavy kettle, and in winter she shivers even beside the stove.

她提起那把沉水壶时两只手会发抖；冬天里，就算站在炉子边她也会打哆嗦。

Her son calls her a burden, but her voice stays tender and mild.

儿子说她是负担，可她的声音依然温柔、和缓。

She keeps one narrow room tidy: a loom, three rolls of grey fabric, and a basket of wool.

她把一间窄小的屋子收拾得整整齐齐：一台织机、三卷灰布，还有一篮羊毛。

Every morning she weaves at the window, and the waves below the cliff keep her time.

每天早晨她都在窗边织布，崖下的海浪替她打着拍子。

When the tide comes in, she stops and watches it climb the stones.

潮水涨上来的时候，她就停下手，看着潮水一点点爬上石头。

Last spring the town held a weaving show, and her cloth was the marvel of the hall.

去年春天，镇上办了织布展，她的布成了整个展厅里的奇观。

The judges called it magnificent and spectacular.

评委说它宏伟壮观。

One of them said it was grand, and another said it was terrific.

其中一个说它了不起，另一个说它棒极了。

A reporter wrote that it was brilliant, even fabulous, work.

一个记者写道，这是绝妙的、甚至是非凡的作品。

She won the first award, and the reward was a small sum of money.

她得了头奖，奖金是一小笔钱。

She felt awkward on the stage, and stood in awe of the crowd.

站在台上她觉得很尴尬，面对人群心里满是敬畏。

The doctors had told her to eat better: more fibre, more vitamins, more real nutrition.

医生早就让她吃得更好一些：多一点纤维，多一点维生素，多一点真正的营养。

She buys soft tissue for her nose and keeps her baggage light when she travels to the clinic.

她买柔软的纸巾擦鼻子；去诊所时，行李也带得很轻。

A young man once said the design was his, but that story was easy to fabricate and easy to prove false.

有个年轻人曾说这个花样是他画的，可这种说法很容易捏造，也很容易被戳穿。

He was a nuisance, she said, and then she laughed.

“他就是个讨厌鬼，”她说，然后笑了。
