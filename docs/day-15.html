The Hives on Bell Island贝尔岛上的蜂箱

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Her grandparents came from the south to reside on Bell Island.

她的祖父母从南方来到这里，选择在贝尔岛定居。

She still inhabits the white house on the hill, and wild bees have their habitat there too.

她至今还住在那座山上的白房子里，野蜂也在那里安家。

It is the only place where that rare kind still lives; three villages away the same bee is extinct.

那是这种稀有蜂种还活着的唯一地方；三个村子之外，同一种蜂已经灭绝了。

Regardless of the cold wind, she walks out at six every morning, because her grandmother taught her to trust her instinct.

不管寒风多大，她每天早上六点都会出门，因为祖母教过她要相信自己的直觉。

Last spring a bee stung her under the eye, and another pierced her glove.

去年春天，一只蜂蜇了她的眼睛下面，另一只刺穿了她的手套。

When a bee thrusts its sting into your skin, you must stand still and wait.

当蜜蜂把螫针扎进你的皮肤时，你必须站着别动，等它自己离开。

A young detective came from the mainland.

一位年轻的侦探从大陆赶来了。

He had to distinguish the sick hives from the healthy ones.

他得把生病的蜂箱和健康的蜂箱区分开。

Each bee, Rosa said, has a distinct pattern on its back and a distinctive smell.

罗莎说，每只蜂背上都有独特的花纹，也各有独特的气味。

She let him observe a frame of comb.

她让他观察一格蜂巢。

The deep reservoir behind the shed holds the water she reserves for the driest weeks.

棚子后面那一大池水库，存着她留给最干旱几周的水。

Four generations of her family have tried to conserve this land, and she means to preserve it.

她家四代人都在努力保护这片土地，她也打算把它保住。

People say she deserves a medal, but she wants the bees to stay.

人们说她该得一枚奖章，可她只想要蜜蜂留下来。

The real problem came from the field next door.

真正的麻烦来自隔壁那块田。

Its owner wanted to protect his crop, so he used a chemical shield against insects.

田主想保住自己的庄稼，就往田里用了一道化学挡箭牌。

Rosa could detect the spray at once.

罗莎立刻就察觉到了空气里的药雾。

Nothing could prevent the bees from dying.

什么也阻止不了蜜蜂一批批死去。

The island council voted to forbid the spray near the flowers.

岛上的议会投票决定禁止在花丛附近喷洒这种药。

The county chose to ban it, and last month the state decided to prohibit it for good.

县里接着决定禁用它，上个月州里又决定永久禁止。

The air near the hives was fragrant, like the perfume her mother wore.

蜂箱边的空气原本是香的，就像她母亲用的香水。

The scent of honey hung over the hill; now the wind carried the smell of poison instead.

蜂蜜的香气过去飘满整座山坡；而如今，风里带的却是毒药的味道。
