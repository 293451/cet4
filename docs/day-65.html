The Table on the Plain平原上的餐桌

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The eating house stands alone on a wide, flat plain, two hours from the sea.

这家小饭馆孤零零地立在平坦的大平原上，离海两小时车程。

Behind the counter hangs an old picture of Marta's mother, painted in 1978.

柜台后面的墙上挂着一张玛尔塔母亲的老照片，是 1978 年画的。

The colours are still vivid, and the face seems to animate the whole room.

颜色依旧鲜艳，那张脸仿佛让整个屋子都活了起来。

Marta says the picture helped the family survive three hard winters.

玛尔塔说，这张照片帮一家人熬过了三个艰难的冬天。

Every spring she tries to revive the recipes written in her mother's notebook.

每年春天，她都试着把母亲笔记本上记下的食谱重新做出来。

There is pork, beef and mutton in the cold room, and seafood on Fridays.

冷藏间里有猪肉、牛肉和羊肉；每到星期五还有海鲜。

She cuts the meat into thin slices and drops them into a hot sauce.

她把肉切成薄片，丢进一锅热腾腾的酱汁里。

On the side she puts a bowl of chips, an onion salad and a plate of snacks.

旁边再摆上一碗薯条、一份洋葱沙拉和一碟小零食。

Guests chew slowly, lick their fingers and wipe them with a paper napkin.

客人慢慢咀嚼，最后舔舔手指，再用纸巾擦干净。

They dine at the long wooden table, and in summer they eat out on a picnic by the lake.

他们在长木桌边吃饭；到了夏天，就跑到湖边野餐。

One July afternoon her grandson dived from a rock into the water.

七月的一个下午，她的外孙从岩石上跳进水里。

It was deep and cold, and for a second he was sure he would drown.

水又深又凉，有一瞬间他确信自己会淹死。

He came up coughing, and rain began to pour on the picnic cloth.

他咳着水浮了上来，雨也开始往野餐布上倾倒。

That year the price of meat plunged, but Marta still complained about the cost.

那年肉价猛跌，可玛尔塔还是抱怨成本太高。

What puzzled the family most was one page of the notebook.

最让一家人想不通的，是笔记本上的某一页。

Nobody could guess what the last line meant.

谁也没法猜出最后一行写的是什么。

Then a young cook from the city made a radical change: he cooked the dish for eight hours.

后来一位城里来的年轻厨师做了一次彻底的改动：他把这道菜煮了八个小时。

Now guests of every racial background drive out from the city for it.

如今各种族背景的客人都会从城里开车来吃这一口。
