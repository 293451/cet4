A Fire in the Old Kitchen老厨房里的一场火

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The cook would activate the old stove, and then react to whatever it gave him.

厨师会点燃那座旧炉子，然后对它给出的一切作出反应。

His methods were exact, and the results were actual, though the whole place felt almost virtual in the dark.

他的手法精确，结果也是实实在在的，尽管这地方在黑暗里几乎像是虚拟的。

A fragment of glass lay on the floor, fragile as a thin sheet.

地板上躺着一块玻璃碎片，脆得像薄薄的一片。

Only a small fraction of the flour could be saved, and just a portion of it was still dry.

只有一小部分面粉能保住，其中又只有一部分还是干的。

The proportion of what was lost to what remained was hard to accept.

损失与留存的比例让人难以接受。

The framework of the shelf was burned, and he could only frown at it.

架子的框架被烧毁了，他只能对着它皱眉。

Then a flame caught, and the fire began to flare and flash across the wall.

接着一簇火焰窜起，火开始沿墙面蔓延、闪烁。

The heat reached his flesh, and he had to refresh himself with cold water.

热浪烫到了他的皮肉，他只得用冷水给自己降温提神。

Yet life would flourish again.

但生命终将再次繁茂。

There was still flour and powder on the top shelf.

顶层架子上还有面粉和粉末。

He was curious about what had happened, and he needed an accurate answer.

他对发生了什么感到好奇，他需要一个准确的答案。

The room was no longer secure, and the cause was obscure.

这个房间不再安全，而原因并不清楚。

He called for help to rescue what he could, but a man was already injured by the smoke.

他呼救去救出能救的东西，但已经有一个人被烟熏伤了。

It was a real danger now, and everything was at stake.

这已成真正的危险，一切都岌岌可危。

For three consecutive days the kitchen stayed shut, and the current situation was still unclear.

厨房连续关了三天，目前情况仍不明朗。
