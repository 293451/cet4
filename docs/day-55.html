The Singing Contest on Millbrook Radio米尔布鲁克电台的歌唱比赛

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Every autumn the radio station in Millbrook proclaims a singing contest, and the town talks for weeks.

每年秋天，米尔布鲁克的电台都会宣布举办歌唱比赛，全镇人会聊上好几个星期。

Last year the host walked on stage and declared that anyone over twelve could enter.

去年，主持人走上台宣布，十二岁以上的人都能报名。

When the first girl finished, the children exclaimed and the old women acclaimed her with long applause.

第一个女孩唱完时，孩子们惊呼起来，老太太们用长长的掌声为她喝彩。

A retired teacher who loved to declaim poems sat as judge, and everyone admired his calm voice.

一位退休教师坐在台下当评委，他喜欢慷慨激昂地朗诵诗，大家都钦佩他那沉稳的嗓音。

Nobody adored a good winner more than he did, and nobody was more jealous of a bad one.

没有人比他更爱慕唱得好的选手，也没有人比他更妒忌唱得差的。

Forty people apply each year, and every applicant sends in a tape.

每年有四十个人申请，每个申请人都得寄一盘录音带。

The rules were short and applicable to all: no drums, and no electric appliance louder than a radio.

规则很短，对所有人都适用：不许用鼓，不许用比收音机更响的电器。

Rosa sent her tape in a shoebox and waited.

罗莎把录音带装在一只鞋盒里寄了出去，然后等着。

Three days later the station sent its reply, and her sister ran to relay the message to their mother in the fields.

三天后电台来了回信，妹妹一路跑到田里，把这个消息转告给妈妈。

The host said Rosa was a reliable singer with a credible voice, and the contest was liable to be her big night.

主持人说罗莎是一位靠得住的歌手，嗓音可信，这场比赛很可能就是她的大日子。

Her friends were green with envy, but Rosa only smiled.

她的朋友们羡慕得眼睛都绿了，罗莎却只是笑了笑。

Her intention was simple: to sing one song her mother would recognise.

她的打算很简单：唱一首妈妈听得懂的歌。

That spring the old grain store was reclaimed and became a studio for the singers.

那年春天，那座旧粮仓被回收改造成了歌手们的排练室。

Every year the number of singers tends to grow; this year the tendency was clear.

每年参赛的人数都趋于增长；而今年这种倾向特别明显。

The new trend was to sing in pairs.

新的风气是两人一组对唱。

The stage was extended, the queue stretched along the road, and the station gave the contest extensive coverage.

舞台扩建了，队伍一直排到马路边，电台对比赛做了大量的报道。

Rosa's father intended to attend, though he used to contend that singing wasted time.

罗莎的父亲打算去参加，尽管他一向认为唱歌是浪费时间。

On the night of the final, her little brother pretended to be a star and sang into a wooden spoon.

决赛那天晚上，她的弟弟假装自己是明星，对着一把木勺唱了起来。
