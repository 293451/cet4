The Long Way to the Villages通往村庄的长路

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The semester was nearly over, and the sports sector of our college was busy.

这个学期快结束了，我们学院的运动部门忙得不可开交。

Every section of the old gym had its own job, and one segment of the floor was kept for yoga.

老健身房每一块区域都有自己的用途，地板的一角留着练瑜伽。

Attendance had been poor all term, so the school made a short-term plan: open the doors and let students move.

整个学期出勤都不好，所以学校定了个短期计划：把门打开，让学生动起来。

Then our coach had a bigger idea.

后来我们的教练有了个更大的主意。

She wanted a real journey, not a cheap tour for tourists.

她想要一次真正的旅程，而不是给游客走的廉价观光。

Years ago she had joined an expedition in the western hills, and the long voyage across the lake had changed her.

几年前她参加过西部山区的一次考察，那段横渡湖面的航行改变了她。

"Take a vacation," she told us, "but do not forget your vocation."

“去度个假吧，”她对我们说，“但别忘了你的职业。”

So we planned a weekend excursion outdoors, a small venture with tents and maps, and we would visit three villages on the way.

于是我们计划了一次周末的户外远足——一次带着帐篷和地图的小小冒险，路上还要走访三个村子。

To get ready, we met in the gym every evening.

为了做好准备，我们每天晚上都在健身房集合。

Some of us did yoga, some did a long workout.

有人练瑜伽，有人做长时间的锻炼。

At first I was not fit at all: after ten minutes my legs felt heavy, and my phone battery lasted longer than my energy.

一开始我一点都不健康：十分钟后我的腿就发沉，手机电池比我的体力还耐用。

But the coach was vigorous and energetic, and her joy was so dynamic that nobody wanted to stop.

但教练精力充沛、干劲十足，她的快乐那么有活力，没人想停下来。

By the end of the month my whole well-being had changed.

到月底，我整个人的身心状态都变了。

On the last evening she asked us to revise our plan.

最后一晚，她让我们修改计划。

She wanted to supervise every detail and to make the whole route visible on a big map.

她要监督每一个细节，还要把整条路线标在一张大得能看见的地图上。

In her vision, we were not simply walking; we were learning how to see.

在她的想象里，我们不只是走路，而是在学着怎么去看。

"Visit a place before you judge it," she said.

“先去一个地方看看，再下判断，”她说。

"Then look again."

“然后再看一次。”
