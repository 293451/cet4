课文

In autumn Lin settled into a flat above a bakery, meaning to dwell there for years.

秋天，林搬进面包房楼上的一间小屋，打算在那儿住上好几年。

Its only asset was the morning light, and the rent was worthwhile.

它唯一的资产是早晨的光线，房租也算值得了。

Then three days of rain came, and every drop on the window left her upset.

接着下了三天的雨，窗上的每一滴都让她心烦。

Water ran down the wall and spread over it like a giant map.

水顺着墙流下来，像一张巨大的地图一样在墙上铺开。

Above her lived Mr. Feng, a gigantic man with a vast voice; his bath was the source.

她楼上住着冯先生，一个巨人般的男人，嗓门极大；源头就是他家的浴室。

The damage was immense and the repair an enormous sum, too much for any tremendous fortune.

损失极大，修理费是一笔庞大的数目，再大的财富也会觉得吃力。

She found the address of the owner on the last page of the contract.

她在合同的最后一页上找到了房主的地址。

She chose to handle it herself, not to tackle it with anger.

她决定自己来处理，而不是带着怒气去对付。

Such a problem is simple to solve but hard to resolve.

这样的问题好解决，却很难真正化解。

Her resolution was one page, and no more.

她的决心是只写一页，不能再多。

Her first two drafts, however, dissolved into complaints about rain and rent.

可她头两稿都化成了对雨水和房租的抱怨。

She made the page precise and concise, in simple words.

于是她把这一页写得准确而简洁，用最简单的词。

She kept the tone normal and cut every subtle hint of blame.

她保持语气平常，删掉了每一处微妙的责怪。

Only one thing was abnormal: the contract printed dissolve as dissove.

只有一件事不正常：合同把 dissolve 印成了 dissove。

She wrote with the utmost care, and signed her full name below.

她极其用心地写，并在下面签了全名。

That week the weather was extreme, and the whole street was wet.

那一周的天气很极端，整条街都是湿的。

The sheer weight of water had broken a pipe; there was no absolute doubt.

纯粹是水的重量压破了管道；这一点毫无疑问。

There was still a real possibility that the owner would refuse to pay.

但房主仍有拒付的可能。

The wall was an utter mess, yet she folded the letter and posted it.

墙上乱成一片，她还是把信折好寄了出去。

Two days later Mr. Feng knocked at her door, quiet and ashamed.

两天后，冯先生来敲门，安静又羞愧。

He paid the whole sum, and the stain was painted white.

他付了全部的钱，那片水渍也被刷白了。

Lin stayed, and learned that a kind, firm page can settle a quarrel.

林留了下来，也明白了一张有分寸的纸能平息一场争吵。
