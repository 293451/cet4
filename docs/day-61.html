The Station on Green Peak绿峰上的气象站

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The weather station sits at the summit of Green Peak, the highest point in the district.

那座气象站坐落在绿峰顶上，是全区最高的一点。

At this altitude the air is thin, and the wind reaches its peak in the afternoon.

在这样的高度，空气稀薄，风在下午达到最强。

For the scientists who live there, sunrise is the climax of every day.

住在那里的科学家看来，日出是一天中的高潮。

This autumn the government devalued the local currency.

今年秋天，政府让本国货币贬值了。

The committee in the city had to cut its expenditure.

城里的委员会不得不削减开支。

Nobody should undervalue a weather station, but fuel was costly, and heating alone expended half of the station's money.

谁也不该低估一座气象站，但燃料很贵，仅取暖一项就花掉了站里一半的钱。

Then an incident changed the plan.

后来，一件事改变了计划。

A casual walker spotted smoke on the north slope.

一个随意的散步者在北坡发现了烟。

It was a small fire, but it had the potential to grow.

火不大，但它有蔓延的潜力。

On that occasion the team had the opportunity to test their new alarm.

那一次，团队有机会试试他们的新警报器。

Their mission was simple: transmit a report from the tower, omit nothing, and submit it to the committee before dark.

他们的任务很简单：从塔上发一份报告，什么都不漏，天黑前提交给委员会。

The old tower emitted a low hum all night.

那座老塔整夜发出低低的嗡鸣。

Its first keeper had been a missionary from the south.

它最早的管理员是从南边来的传教士。

"We must not dismiss small signals," he used to say.

“小小的信号也不能不理，”他过去常说。

Later, the chief admitted that the station needed money and promised to commit next year's budget to repairs.

后来，站长承认站里需要钱，答应把明年的预算拨一部分来维修。

On the radio, the news spoke of a missile test far to the north.

收音机里，新闻正说着北方远处的一次导弹试验。

In the valley below, farmers had stopped using the strongest pesticide, and the incidence of illness among children had fallen.

下面的山谷里，农民已经不再用最强的杀虫剂，孩子们的发病率也降了下来。

A health report said that suicides in the district were fewer than ten years ago.

一份健康报告说，这一带的自杀人数比十年前少了。

A small keypad guards the station door.

一个小小的密码键盘守着气象站的门。

The password changes every month, and only four people know it.

密码每个月换一次，只有四个人知道。

"Everything up here is old," the chief says, "except the password."

“这里什么都老，”站长说，“除了密码。”
