The Box in the Museum Basement博物馆地下室的箱子

0:00 / 0:00

课文

In a small museum by the sea, Elsa worked in the restoration department.

在海边的一座小博物馆里，艾尔莎在修复部门工作。

After three days of rain, water stood in the basement, and the shelves could no longer hold their load.

连下三天雨后，地下室积了水，架子再也承受不住上面的负重了。

At dawn two men came to unload the wet boxes before they had to depart for the next town.

天刚亮，两个男人来把湿箱子卸下来，因为他们还得出发去下一个镇子。

Elsa lifted a cloth and began to uncover a wooden box.

艾尔莎揭开一块布，开始打开一只木箱。

She unfolded the paper inside, and it revealed a grey substance that no one could name.

她展开里面的纸，露出来一种谁也说不清名字的灰色物质。

The box had to withstand damp air for years, and it did.

这只箱子必须常年扛得住潮气，而它确实扛住了。

From a distant shelf she brought her notebook.

她从远处的一层架子上取来了自己的笔记本。

The weather there was never constant, and her habits were consistent: the same lamp, the same gloves, the same slow hands.

那里的天气从来都不稳定，但她的习惯始终如一：同一盏灯，同一副手套，同样缓慢的双手。

Her apartment stood two streets apart from the museum.

她的公寓离博物馆只隔着两条街。

On the way home she kept thinking of a particle of dust on the paper, and the answer came in an instant.

回家的路上，她一直想着纸上的一粒灰尘，答案忽然在一瞬间出现了。

It was not instantaneous, but close.

这个答案不是瞬时的，但也差不远了。

Her note to her teacher was prompt.

她写给老师的便条回得很及时。

Two colleagues held different views.

两位同事看法不同。

One had a partial view and would not listen.

一位抱着片面的看法，谁的话都听不进去。

The other had a bias against everything found in the sea.

另一位对一切从海里捞出来的东西都抱有偏见。

Elsa stayed neutral; she wanted the truth.

艾尔莎保持中立；她只想要真相。

That winter her teacher fell ill.

那年冬天，她的老师病倒了。

The pain was acute and the news severe.

疼痛剧烈，消息也很严重。

The doctor said his state was grave, and that worry would only aggravate it.

医生说他情况严峻，还说担心只会让病情加重。

Elsa brought soup to alleviate the pain and read to him to relieve the long afternoons.

艾尔莎给他送汤来减轻疼痛，给他念书来缓解那些漫长的下午。

His family tried not to grieve too early.

他的家人尽量不让自己过早地陷入悲痛。

When the box was opened, the town gave her a grant to study the grey substance.

箱子最终被打开时，镇上给了她一笔拨款，让她研究那种灰色物质。

Elsa was grateful.

艾尔莎很感激。

She wrote in her notebook: some things are only found slowly.

她在笔记本上写下一行字：有些东西，只能慢慢找到。
