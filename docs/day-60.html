The Tractor in the Rain雨中的拖拉机

0:00 / 0:00

课文

On a wet Tuesday in October, a farmer drove his old tractor into Nina's repair shed.

十月里一个下雨的星期二，一位农民把旧拖拉机开进了妮娜的修理棚。

"It is impossible to finish today," she said.

“今天修不完，”她说。

"The probable problem is the fuel pump, and the only one available is in the city."

“最可能出问题的是燃油泵，而唯一能弄到的泵在城里。”

Her father said patience prevails in a small workshop, so she told him to come on Friday.

她父亲说过，小铺子里靠的是耐心，于是她让他星期五再来。

While he waited, Nina read the contract he had signed with the co-op.

他等着的时候，妮娜读了他跟合作社签的那份合同。

She subtracted the price of oil, deducted the cost of two tires, and reduced the total to fit his budget.

她减去油钱，扣掉两条轮胎的费用，再把总价压下来，好配得上他那点预算。

It took an hour to extract the pump from the engine.

把油泵从发动机里取出来，花了她一个小时。

A lamp above the bench attracted moths, and the radio distracted her.

工作台上方的一盏灯招来了飞蛾，收音机也让她分心。

On the wall hung her father's abstract drawings of machines.

墙上挂着她父亲画的机器图纸，都是些抽象的东西。

Her fingers were stiff, and every movement felt clumsy.

她的手指发僵，每个动作都显得笨拙。

Her gloves were not flexible enough, so she used an elastic band and a sheet of plastic.

手套不够灵活，她只好用一根松紧带和一张塑料片凑合。

The wet metal reflected the grey sky back at her.

湿漉漉的金属把灰白的天空反射回她脸上。

"Nothing here is permanent," her father had said, "not even this shed."

“这里没有什么是永久的，”她父亲说过，“连这间棚子也不是。”

But the old tractor looked eternal to her: thirty years in the same field, in the same rain.

可那台旧拖拉机在她眼里却是永恒的：在同一个田里、同一场雨里，待了三十年。

She worked alone: a single pair of hands, the sole worker in the shed.

她一个人干活：一双手，是棚子里唯一的工人。

A couple of neighbours stopped by.

几个邻居顺路过来看。

"You are merely one person," one of them said.

“你只不过是一个人，”其中一个说。

"You cannot do double work."

“你干不了双份的活。”

But the reward was duple: the money and the farmer's smile.

但回报是双重的：钱，还有农民的笑。

There was something singular in that.

这里面有种不同寻常的东西。

On Friday the farmer asked her to evaluate the tractor.

星期五，农民请她给这台拖拉机估个价。

She wrote a number on a piece of paper.

她在一张纸上写了个数字。

"It is more valuable repaired than sold," she said.

“修好了比卖掉更值钱，”她说。
