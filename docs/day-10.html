The Shop at the Edge of Town镇边的小店

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Rosa kept a small shop at the edge of town.

罗莎在镇子边上开着一家小店。

Behind it was a narrow lawn and two rose bushes.

店后有一小片草坪和两丛玫瑰。

Beyond the fence the weeds had grown into a jungle, and every Saturday she would take her brush and work until dark.

篱笆外面，野草已经长成了一片丛林，每个星期六她都会拿上刷子干到天黑。

Past the last bush lay an open field where village boys played football.

最后一丛灌木过去，就是一片空地，村里的男孩们在踢球。

Her nephew Tom was an accountant in the city.

她的侄子汤姆是城里的会计。

One summer he came to check her accounts.

有一年夏天，他回来查她的账。

He sat at the counter and began to count.

他坐在柜台边，开始数。

Some of the bills could not be added up; the costs were almost uncountable.

有些账单根本加不起来，那些开销几乎数不清。

He asked her to delete three old records.

他让她删掉三条旧记录。

Then he found a discount she had given to a customer and never written down.

接着他发现了一笔她给过顾客、却从没记下来的折扣。

"You took a loan from the bank," he said, "and the debt is still on the paper."

“你从银行贷了款，”他说，“这笔债还挂在纸上。”

"You must repay it before winter."

“冬天之前你必须还清。”

Rosa looked at the bill on the wall and said nothing.

罗莎看着墙上的那张账单，什么也没说。

That year the shop had to undergo big changes.

那一年，小店不得不经历大变化。

Rosa had to endure many long nights.

罗莎不得不熬过许多个长夜。

She bought durable shelves and kept the store open for a longer duration.

她买了耐用的货架，让店每天开得更久。

Her back suffered, but she never gave up.

她的背很受罪，但她从没放弃。

From the numbers in front of him, Tom could infer one thing: the shop was not dying.

从眼前的这些数字里，汤姆能推断出一件事：这家店没有死。

He referred to the neighbours who came every single day.

他提到了那些每天都来的老邻居。

On Sunday he met an old counterpart of his aunt, a shopkeeper from the next street, and Rosa encountered an old friend at last.

星期天，汤姆遇到了姑姑的一位老同行——隔壁街的另一位店主，而罗莎也终于遇见了一位老朋友。

That afternoon a referee blew his whistle on the field.

那天下午，一位裁判在球场上吹响了哨子。

A senator and two members of the town council came to confer a small award on the oldest shop in town.

一位参议员和两名镇议会的成员来了，为镇上最老的店铺颁发一个小奖项。

Rosa stood at her counter and smiled.

罗莎站在柜台后面，笑了。
