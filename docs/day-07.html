The Lighthouse Keeper守灯塔的人

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The keeper had to adopt a routine no one else would want.

守塔人不得不养成一种别人不愿要的作息。

In his free hours he had little to amuse him, and the sea could still amaze a man who had watched it for forty years.

空闲时没什么能让他消遣，而大海仍能让一个看了它四十年的人惊叹。

A sudden wave might startle him, but that was his only pastime and his only recreation.

一个突如其来的浪头也许能吓他一跳，但那是他唯一的消遣，也是唯一的娱乐。

His choices were not randomly made, and never arbitrary.

他的选择不是随便做的，也从不随心所欲。

He could alter the lamp, or he could find an alternative: to shift the lens a little and let the beam turn.

他可以把灯改一改，或者找另一种办法：把镜片挪一点，让光柱转起来。

The change was swift.

这个变化很迅速。

Weather in these parts will vary, and one thing was variable while another stayed invariably the same.

这一带天气会变，有一样可变，另一样却始终不变。

The little model boat on his shelf was the only thing he would modify, and his needs were modest.

架子上那只小船模型是他唯一会改动的东西，而他的需求很有限。

He was earnest about the work, and moderate in his habits, ready to tolerate anything but a lazy hand.

他对这份活计很认真，起居有节制，什么都能忍，就是忍不了一双懒手。

Light can be slight, or it can enlighten a whole coast.

光可以很微弱，也可以照亮整条海岸。

During lightning and thunder he would stay at the window with delight, never in plight, never with blight.

电闪雷鸣时他会欣喜地守在窗前，从不陷入困境，也从不消沉。

At dawn the lamp would alight once more.

天亮时，灯又会亮起。
