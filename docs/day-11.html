The Whisper and the Deadline那句耳语与最后的截稿日

课文

Green Harbor was once a fertile town.

绿港曾经是个肥沃的小镇。

From the ferry you could see its skyline of white mill towers, and every shop on Main Street had a name I knew by heart.

从渡轮上就能看见它那一片白色磨坊塔楼的天际线，主街上每一家店的名字我都记得。

I worked at the local paper, where the old editor would outline the next morning's pages on a board, underline the two shops that still bought ads, and keep the same layout for forty years.

我在当地报社干活，老编辑会在板子上勾勒出第二天报纸的版面，给那两家还在买广告的店铺画上横线，而且四十年来版面布局一直没变。

Most readers prefer it that way.

大多数读者更喜欢这样。

But the town was going bad.

可是小镇在变坏。

Rain had eroded the river bank, the mill walls had begun to decay, and empty houses were falling to ruin.

雨水侵蚀了河岸，磨坊的墙开始腐朽，空房子正一点点变成废墟。

Cheap imported cloth spoiled the market, and the harbour road deteriorated year after year.

便宜的进口布料毁了市场，港口那条路一年比一年糟。

Our ideas were stale, our headlines outdated, and every small loss seemed to undermine the trust that should underlie a local paper.

我们的想法陈旧了，标题也过时了，每一次小小的损失似乎都在削弱那份本该支撑一份地方报纸的信任。

Then a whisper reached my desk.

后来一句耳语传到了我的办公桌前。

The mill owner had bribed two officials and cheated the town, hiding a deficit he never admitted.

磨坊主贿赂了两名官员，欺骗了全镇，还藏着一笔他从不肯承认的赤字。

He had tried to deceive us all.

他试图蒙骗我们所有人。

I spent a week trying to determine who had signed the papers.

我花了一个星期想查明那些文件到底是谁签的。

That spring the mill went bankrupt, and the men who took the money turned out to be corrupt.

那年春天磨坊破产了，收钱的那些人后来被查出是腐败的。

When I published the story, a protest erupted outside our office, and the mayor's speech was interrupted twice.

报道登出来后，一场抗议在我们报社门口爆发，市长的讲话被打断了两次。

The scandal disrupted the whole town for months.

这桩丑闻扰乱了整个小镇好几个月。

My editor asked me what I had perceived and what plan I could conceive for the paper's future.

编辑问我察觉到了什么，又能为报社的将来构想出什么办法。

I had no answer; my mind went blank.

我答不上来，脑子一片空白。

But the deadline was Friday, and I had to write something.

可截稿日是星期五，我总得写点什么。

01 fertile /'f3:rtl/ adj. 能结果的；肥沃的
课文Green Harbor was once a fertile town
记《西游记》里有一段是偷吃人参 果的场景，树上结了很多的人参果，类似佛头 (fertile的发音)，所以那棵树是“能结果的；肥 沃的”。
根fer(带来) + tile → 能带来果实 → 肥沃的
真题In our always-connected world, boredom may be a hard-to-define state, but it is a fertile one. Watch paint dry or water boil, or at least put away your smartphone for a while, and you might unlock your next big idea. 在我们这 个始终互联的世界里，无聊可能是一种很难定 义的状态，但它是一种有产出的状态。看着油 漆变干或水沸腾，或者至少把你的智能手机收 起来一段时间，你可能会解锁你的下一个大 创意。
族fertility n. 肥沃 / fertilizer n. 肥料
