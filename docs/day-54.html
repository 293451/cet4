The Painter at the Checkpoint检查站上的画匠

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Lena works at a mountain checkpoint between two countries.

莉娜在两国交界的一座山口检查站工作。

Each morning she checks the papers of the people who pass the barrier.

每天早上，她都检查过关人的证件。

From her standpoint, the job is never boring.

从她的立场看，这份工作一点也不无聊。

A good officer needs considerable patience and must be considerate to tired families with children.

一名好哨兵需要相当的耐心，还得体谅带着孩子的疲惫家庭。

That was the first point her captain made, and she still remembers his consideration.

这是队长第一天对她说的第一个要点，她至今记得他这份周到的考虑。

At night, in a room behind the checkpoint, Lena paints.

夜里，在检查站后面的一间小屋里，莉娜画画。

She has a fantasy about showing her work in a big city; the notion feels foolish, but she cannot drop it.

她幻想有一天能在大城市展出自己的画；这念头显得很傻，可她就是放不下。

The mountains fascinate her: at sunrise they look like a dream, more fancy than any picture she could imagine.

群山让她着迷：日出时它们像一场梦，比她想象的任何画面都更绚丽。

Yet she knows the beautiful light on the rock is partly an illusion, and the image changes with every hour.

可她知道，岩石上那美丽的光有一部分只是错觉，那幅影像每小时都在变。

Her teacher, an old philosopher, used to say that art is infinite and that nobody can confine it to a definite rule.

她的老师是位老哲学家，过去常说艺术是无限的，谁也没法把它限制在一条明确的规则里。

He asked her to define beauty, then to clarify it, then to refine it until only the true part was left.

他让她先给美下定义，再把它讲清楚，然后一遍遍提炼，直到只剩下真实的那一部分。

"Painting is not a profession," her father said.

“画画不是个职业，”她父亲说。

Her teacher said something else: one small idea must never occupy your whole mind, or your occupation becomes a cage.

老师说的却是另一回事：一个小小的念头绝不能占据你的整个脑子，否则你的职业就成了笼子。

Last spring a firm in the city confirmed that it wanted to buy three of her paintings and sent a cheque in the post.

去年春天，城里一家公司确认要买她三幅画，还从邮局寄来了一张支票。

The news seemed to affirm everything she had believed about herself.

这个消息仿佛证实了她一直悄悄相信的一切。

Her friends claim she was simply lucky, but she will not confess to luck.

朋友们都说她只是运气好，可她不肯承认这是运气。

When a reporter asked her to explain her success, she said one thing:

当记者让她解释自己的成功时，她只说了一件事：

"I looked at the same mountain for ten years."

“我盯着同一座山看了十年。”
