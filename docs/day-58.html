The Torn Page in the Lane House巷子老屋里那页破纸

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Old Mr. Gu died in the lane house, leaving two sons.

老顾先生在那栋巷房里去世，留下两个儿子。

Gu Ping found a torn document: a list of the family property.

顾平找到一份撕破的文件：一张家产清单。

Gu An read it for a fortnight, called it forged, and said it would offend their father.

顾安读了两个星期，说这是伪造的，还说这会冒犯他们的父亲。

By custom, the younger son got only a range of rooms behind the kitchen.

按巷子里的规矩，小儿子只分到厨房后面的几间小屋。

Gu Ping arranged the funeral: the sequence of the speeches and the series of small events.

顾平操办了葬礼：致辞的顺序，以及那一连串细小的环节。

Frequent questions tired him, and at the final reading he stepped back.

频繁的追问让他疲惫，念到最后一段时，他往后退了一步。

At last he admitted the line was his, written to defend his mother's piece of the estate.

最后他承认那行字是他写的，为的是保住母亲那份遗产。

The primary rule was that a brother must never be shamed in public; the secondary matter had to wait.

首要的规矩是：兄弟绝不能被当众羞辱；那件次要的事只能往后放。

Aunt Feng stood by the fence to settle the quarrel.

冯阿姨站在篱笆边，来平息这场争吵。

A family is like a yard, she said: guard the property inside, but throw stones around and you break your own fence.

她说，一个家就像一个院子：里面的财产要守，可到处扔石头，砸断的就是自己的篱笆。

Her words were so appropriate that both brothers bowed, and the lane stood in perfect propriety.

她的话如此恰当，兄弟俩都低下了头，整条巷子举止得体。

'The wealth of this family is not in the rooms,' she said.

“这个家的财富不在那些屋子里，”她说。

Ownership, Gu An saw, is not paper but a promise.

顾安明白了，所有权不是纸张，而是一个承诺。

He had come to possess the house and left with something worth more.

他本是回来占这栋房子的，走时却带走了更值得的东西。

As a preliminary step, the brothers carried out a chair, a bed and a chain.

作为第一步，兄弟俩抬出一把椅子、一张床，还有一条链子。

The house had been primitive: one bowl, one lamp, one stove.

这栋房子一直很简陋：一只碗、一盏灯、一个炉子。

Every subsequent question about the will still had to be answered.

关于遗嘱的随后每一个问题，仍然必须回答。

It was the last of a series of events in that lane.

那是那条巷子里一连串事件中的最后一件。

In his prime the old man was a giant of the lane; the snow came as a consequence.

在盛年时，老人曾是巷子里的一个巨人；那场雪，就是随之而来的结果。

The document was eventually framed on the wall.

那份文件最终被装进框里，挂在了墙上。
