The Girl Who Copied the Old Letters抄旧信的女孩

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Red Ash is a small district at the foot of an old volcano.

红灰镇是一座小镇，坐落在一座老火山的山脚下。

Mira works in a newspaper office there: one dim room, one wide window, and an iron column by the door.

米拉在那儿的一家报社上班：一间昏暗的屋子，一扇宽窗，门边立着一根铁柱子。

Fifty years ago a disaster hit the town.

五十年前，一场灾难袭击了这座小镇。

After the eruption came a famine and long months of distress, and the labour of rebuilding fell on every family.

火山喷发之后是饥荒和长达数月的悲痛，重建的劳动落在每一户人家身上。

Mira's grandfather was a diligent reporter then, and he wrote down everything he saw.

米拉的祖父当年是个勤劳的记者，他把自己看到的一切都记了下来。

The town still keeps his letters, but his handwriting is thin and vague, and most readers cannot follow it.

镇上至今留着他的信件，可他的笔迹又细又模糊，多数读者根本读不下去。

So Mira's job is to imitate it.

于是米拉的工作就是模仿这笔迹。

She does not disguise her own name; she simulates the old letters only for one weekly column.

她并不伪装自己的名字；她只为每周的一个专栏模拟这些旧信。

She taps the paper, pats it flat, and copies each line with a pen that is always handy.

她轻敲纸面，把它拍平，用一支总在手边的笔一行行抄写。

For convenience, the letters live in a box beside her desk, and her first draft is never clean.

为了方便，这些信就放在她桌旁的箱子里，而她的初稿从来都不干净。

Mira is keen on this work, and passion keeps her at the desk for hours.

米拉热爱这份工作，热情让她在桌前坐上好几个小时。

Her editor spurs her on, and readers' letters stimulate her.

编辑鞭策她，读者的来信激励她。

Yet she is doubtful about one thing: can she convince anyone that a famine is more than numbers?

可有一件事她始终心存疑虑：她能让人相信饥荒不只是几个数字吗？

Small things disturb her.

小事也会打扰她。

The heavy smell of old oil fills her with disgust.

旧机油的气味让她满心厌恶。

When the lamp burns low, she cannot judge the dimensions of her own words.

灯芯烧低的时候，她辨不出自己笔下字句的尺寸。

Some evenings she sits still and watches the smoke drift over the roofs, toward a destination she can only guess.

有些傍晚，她静静地坐着，看烟从屋顶上飘过，飘向一个她只能猜测的目的地。
