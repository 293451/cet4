The Glove with a Paper Tag带纸吊牌的手套

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Mei lived in a small household in the north, and she was a housewife who sewed gloves in her garage every afternoon.

梅住在北方一个小小的家庭里，是个家庭主妇，每天下午都在自家车库里缝手套。

Her gloves had no brand and no label, only a paper tag with her own name on it.

她的手套没有牌子，也没有标签，只有一张写着她自己名字的纸吊牌。

She had an ambition: to win a patent for the soft lining she had invented.

她有一个抱负：为自己发明的柔软内衬拿到一项专利。

Bigger factories had money and lawyers, so she always seemed to lag behind.

大厂有钱、有律师，所以她好像总是落后一步。

Still, she kept her own pace and never missed a day of work.

但她保持着自己的节奏，一天工都没落下。

The gap between her table and theirs was wide, but she had one advantage: patience.

她的工作台和大厂之间的差距很大，可她有一个优势：耐心。

That autumn the town's orchestra lost its old conductor, and the musicians asked her to consult on their uniforms.

那年秋天，镇上的管弦乐队失去了老指挥，乐手们请她就制服的事做个咨询。

The new conductor blew his whistle, raised his hand, and the players tuned their instruments.

新指挥吹响哨子，抬起手，乐手们给乐器调音。

Mei sat in the back row, and a tune she had known since childhood filled the hall.

梅坐在最后一排，一首她从小就熟悉的曲子充满了整个大厅。

The leader of a famous company came to the concert as the host of the evening.

一家著名公司的老板作为当晚的东道主来看演出。

He was generous with praise in public, but hostile to her in private.

他在人前慷慨地夸人，私下里却对她充满敌意。

"Your gloves are poor work," he said, and the insult made her face hot with rage.

“你这手套做得不行。”他说，这句侮辱让她脸上冒火，怒火直往上冲。

On the way home she felt sore all over, and by midnight a headache woke her.

回家的路上她浑身酸痛，到了半夜又头疼醒来。

She took a tablet and a pill, but at two in the morning her son called an ambulance.

她吃了一片药片和一颗药丸，可凌晨两点，她儿子叫了救护车。

At the hospital a young doctor wore gloves and told her she needed rest.

在医院，一位年轻医生戴着手套，告诉她需要休息。

Three weeks later a newspaper headline read: "A Housewife's Gloves Cross the Continent."

三周后，报纸上一条大字标题写着：“一位家庭主妇的手套跨越大洲。”

The company headquarters in Europe had seen her little paper tag in a photo.

欧洲的公司总部在一张照片里看见了她那张小小的纸吊牌。

They offered to buy her design, and this time she said yes.

他们提出买下她的设计，这一次，她答应了。
