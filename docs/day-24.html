The Notebook in the Cabinet柜子里的那本笔记

0:00 / 0:00

课文

The old cabin stood at the edge of the village, beside a field where cattle grazed.

那间旧小木屋立在村子的边上，旁边是一片放牛的草地。

Inside, a wooden cabinet held twenty years of letters, and a thick cable ran from the roof down to the road.

屋里有一个木柜子，装着二十年的信件；一根粗电缆从屋顶一直拉到路边。

In the square there was a stone statue of Nora's grandfather.

广场上有一尊诺拉祖父的石像。

A decade ago he had been a man of high status in the town.

十年前，他是镇上地位很高的人。

In previous years, and even prior to that, his first priority had been the school.

在以前那些年里，甚至更早的时候，他的头等大事一直是学校。

He had fought for liberty all his life.

他一生都在为自由而战。

He helped liberate the village from a bad landlord, and every choice he made was deliberate.

他帮着把村子从坏地主手里解放出来，他做的每一个选择都是深思熟虑的。

Nora found a notebook in the cabinet.

诺拉在柜子里找到了一本笔记本。

The author was her grandfather himself.

作者就是她祖父本人。

The town authority had never read it.

镇上的官方从来没有读过它。

The pages were authentic and genuine, and inside them was the mind of a quiet genius.

那些纸页是真实而真挚的，里面装着一颗安静的天才的心。

Her own writing, she felt, was inferior to his.

她觉得自己的文字比不上他的。

But his style was superior, its superiority was clear, and a few pages were superb.

但他的风格更胜一筹，他的优越之处一望而知，还有几页写得极为精彩。

Still, no one is supreme.

不过，没有人是至高无上的。

She wanted the whole community to read the notebook.

她想让整个社区都读到这本笔记。

The nearest telecommunications tower stood two blocks away, so she put the pages online.

最近的电信塔就在两个街区外，于是她把那些纸页放到了网上。

Her neighbours were sociable and easy-going.

她的邻居们好交际又随和。

They began to associate the old man with the town again, and the friendship between Nora and the village was mutual.

他们又开始把这位老人和这座小镇联系在一起，诺拉和村子之间的情谊是相互的。
