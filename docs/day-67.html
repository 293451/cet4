The Bird Hospital on Wild Island野生岛上的鸟医院

0:00 / 0:00

课文

Wild Island lies far overseas, two hundred kilometres from the mainland.

野生岛远在海外，离大陆两百公里。

A federal agency governs the island; the Ministry of the Environment pays for the hospital.

一个联邦机构管理这座岛；鸟医院的经费由环境部出。

The hospital is a broad wooden building with a board by the gate.

医院是一栋宽阔的木屋，门口立着一块牌子。

Every spring volunteers assemble from all over the country and come aboard at the coast.

每年春天，全国各地的志愿者聚到一起，在岸边登上渡船。

A panel of experts visits twice a year; an administrative staff keeps the records.

一个专家组每年来访两次；一名行政人员管着所有记录。

Dr. Shen, the vet, has worked here thirty years and leads the team.

兽医沈医生在这里工作了三十年，是这个团队的头儿。

She studied abroad for three years before returning to the island.

她曾在国外学习三年，之后才回到岛上。

She has a dry wit and a wise, sane way with frightened visitors.

她说话风趣，面对受惊的来客时既明智又清醒。

She administers the medicine herself and keeps a lengthy record of every case.

她亲手给鸟喂药，还为每一个病例写下冗长的记录。

The first symptom is usually a wrong turn in flight.

最初的症状通常是飞行时转错了方向。

At dusk the gulls circle clockwise around the lighthouse before they land.

黄昏时，海鸥会绕着灯塔顺时针盘旋，然后才落下。

A weak bird is often a symbol of something wrong in the sea itself.

一只虚弱的鸟，往往是大海本身出了问题的标志。

The old lighthouse symbolizes the island better than anything else.

这座老灯塔，比岛上任何东西都更能象征这座岛。

The staff treat every bird with mercy and feel sympathy for the fishermen whose nets cause the injuries.

员工们对每一只鸟都心怀仁慈，也真心同情那些渔网伤了鸟的渔民。

Their work is not a symphony, but on quiet mornings the birds make something close to one.

他们的工作不是交响乐；但在安静的早晨，鸟叫出来的东西很像一首。

In warm years the season lengthens by a week or two.

暖年里，这个季节会延长一两个星期。

A deep breath here is enough to fill your chest with salt.

在这里深吸一口气，胸口就全是咸味。

The volunteers are alike in one way: none of them can sit still.

志愿者们有一点是相同的：谁也没法安安静静坐着。

Likewise, the birds return to the same nest every year.

同样地，这些鸟每年都回到同一个巢。

The state plans to widen the channel next year.

政府计划明年把航道加宽。

A young visitor wrote in the book: "Cold, wet, and the best week of my life."

一位年轻访客在门口的册子上写道：“又冷又湿，却是我这辈子最好的一周。”

Those words led other students to the island.

这些话把更多学生引到了岛上。
